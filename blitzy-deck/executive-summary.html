Risk Severity Mitigation

Three unattended ir.cron jobs Medium Idempotent, batched logic (follow-up caps ≤500 partners/run); reachable under Settings → Technical → Automation
Per-story coverage interpretation Low Per-module aggregate is the meaningful gate — AAM 87% / ABM 89% / ADR 87% / APF 90%, all passing
Multi-company record-rule exposure Medium Twelve company_id-scoped ir.rule records across the four newest addons; ACL coverage verified
sudo() elevation breadth Low Exactly one executable production .sudo() — an audited ir.config_parameter read, not a write
Demo-data independence Low Newest addons ship no demo/; tests build their own fixtures and install under --without-demo
