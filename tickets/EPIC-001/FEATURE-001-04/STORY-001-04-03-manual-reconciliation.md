## User Story

**As a** Chief Accountant

**I want** one workspace for every statement line the rules of `STORY-001-04-02` left behind on a named company's **Bank** journal — a candidate search I narrow by partner, by an amount range and by a date range; a full match against one journal item or many; a part allocation that settles what the payment covers and leaves the rest of the item open at a stated residual; an accepted difference directed to a named account inside the reconciliation; a whole journal entry created from inside the reconciliation for a line no existing item explains; and the reversal of any one of those decisions on its own

**So that** the 50 exception lines remaining from the 1,000 imported for `US-01` **Global Holdings Inc.** over 2025-03-01 to 2025-03-31 are resolved line by line rather than left as a remainder, and a cleared period's **Bank Reconciliation Statement** — asserted over the fixture range 2024-02-01 to 2024-02-29 — reads an unreconciled statement lines total of `USD 0.00` tying to a Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 balance of `USD 0.00` at a difference of `USD 0.00`, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01 — with every posting I make carrying total debits equal to total credits, every residual I leave behind stated as an exact amount rather than left to be inferred, and every reversal recorded with the acting Chief Accountant, the timestamp and the state before the change, so the External Auditor tests my judgement against a written trail instead of re-performing the reconciliation, and the period close in FEATURE-001-07 begins against a proved Bank 1010 balance rather than waiting on one

## Acceptance Criteria

Manual reconciliation is one capability, not two. A line whose amount ties exactly and a line whose amount ties only in part are worked in the same place, with the same candidate search, by the same role, and both end in a posting through the same **Bank** journal — the difference between them is whether the item the payment was applied to reaches a residual of `USD 0.00` or stays open at a stated figure. All 7 criteria below are written against one deterministic artifact set, so every assertion has exactly one pass-or-fail reading. The set is fixed by the parent feature and by the Epic's canonical registers and is not restated per criterion:

|----------|-------|------------------------|

A journal item belonging to any company other than the one whose **Bank** journal is being reconciled is never offered as a candidate against it |

**Coverage distribution.** Criteria 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8 are the valid-input path — a filtered candidate search, a full match against three journal items, a part allocation leaving a stated residual, an accepted difference written off in a balanced entry, and a whole entry created from inside the reconciliation for a line no journal item explains. Criterion 5 is the reversal path, which the Epic's merge record obliges this story to carry as a criterion rather than as an assumption. Criterion 6 is the invalid and incomplete input case and is also the error-handling case, written as one confirmation asserted over four Examples rows because each leaves the identical invariant behind: nothing written. Criterion 7 is the accounting edge case, carrying the foreign-currency part allocation with its rate basis, its three recorded currency amounts and its exchange difference. Criterion 8 closes with the report tie-out that proves the period was cleared item by item rather than balanced by a plug, and it carries the CF-003 obligation the parent feature places on this story.

### Scenario 1: A filtered candidate search returns only the open items of the reconciling company, ranked by closeness of amount

- **Given** the unreconciled statement line of company `US-01` **Global Holdings Inc.** dated 2024-02-20, bank reference `VND-0220`, narration "Vendor Payment", amount `−USD 890.25`, counterparty "Hardware Supplies Co", on the `US-01` **Bank** journal, carrying the provisional posting `STORY-001-04-01` created against Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 and claimed by no reconciliation rule in `STORY-001-04-02`; and one open vendor bill of `US-01` for that partner on Accounts Payable 2000 with a residual of `USD 890.25`; and open journal items of `NL-01` **Global Europe SARL** for the same partner within the same amount range — each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Chief Accountant filters the reconciliation candidates of that statement line by the partner "Hardware Supplies Co", by an amount range of `USD 800.00` to `USD 900.00` and by a date range of 2024-02-01 to 2024-02-29
- **Then** the candidate list presents each open journal item of `US-01` with its account code and its own residual amount in USD to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01; the open vendor bill on Accounts Payable 2000 with a residual of `USD 890.25` is present in that list; the list is ordered by closeness of the candidate residual to the statement line amount of `USD 890.25` and then, among candidates equally close, by ascending date, so that two searches over the same candidate set return the same order; every candidate returned belongs to `US-01`, and the count of returned candidates belonging to `NL-01` or to any company other than `US-01` is 0, because a journal item from another entity's books is never offered against this entity's **Bank** journal; the search returns within 1 second against a **Bank** journal of `US-01` holding 100 unreconciled journal items and its results are paginated; and the statement line's reconciled state is still false with 0 journal entries created, because a search commits nothing

### Scenario 2: A full match against three journal items closes each of them under one matching number

- **Given** an additional seeded statement line on the same `US-01` **Bank** journal for `+USD 1,500.00`, unreconciled and carrying the provisional posting against Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099; and three open customer invoices of `US-01` for one partner on Accounts Receivable 1200 with residuals of `USD 400.00`, `USD 500.00` and `USD 600.00`; and the fiscal period containing that line open for `US-01` with no journal-entries lock date in force on or after its date — each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Chief Accountant selects all three invoices against that statement line and confirms the reconciliation
- **Then** each of the three invoices reaches a residual of `USD 0.00`; the statement line's reconciled state becomes true and its own residual becomes `USD 0.00`; the statement line and the three invoices share one matching number, so the four records are readable as one settlement rather than as four unrelated events; and the resulting entry posts through the `US-01` **Bank** journal as debit Bank 1010 `USD 1,500.00` against credit Accounts Receivable 1200 `USD 1,500.00`, so total debits of `USD 1,500.00` equal total credits of `USD 1,500.00` at a difference of `USD 0.00`, the Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 line of the provisional posting clearing to `USD 0.00` — with each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01, and the allocation of `USD 400.00`, `USD 500.00` and `USD 600.00` across the three invoices summing to the `USD 1,500.00` of the line at a difference of `USD 0.00`

### Scenario 3: A part allocation leaves a stated residual, assigns no matching number and keeps the item open

- **Given** an additional seeded statement line on the same `US-01` **Bank** journal for `+USD 500.00`, unreconciled; and one open customer invoice of `US-01` on Accounts Receivable 1200 with a residual of `USD 1,000.00` for the same partner — each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Chief Accountant allocates that statement line against that invoice as a part payment
- **Then** one partial reconciliation record links the statement line's Accounts Receivable 1200 counterpart to the invoice's Accounts Receivable 1200 line for `USD 500.00` in the company currency of `US-01`; the invoice residual becomes `USD 500.00` and the invoice stays open, so the collectable balance the aged receivable presentation reads is `USD 500.00` rather than nil; the statement line is fully consumed at a residual of `USD 0.00` and a reconciled state of true; the count of full-reconciliation records created is 0 and no matching number is assigned to the invoice while it remains open, because a matching number states that a set of items is settled and this one is not; the invoice's allocation history lists that allocation with its amount of `USD 500.00`, its allocation date and the remaining balance of `USD 500.00`, and where more than one allocation is present it also states the latest matched date across them; and the entry posted through the `US-01` **Bank** journal carries total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `USD 0.00` — with each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01. The same shape holds for the parent feature's worked line `L-0330`, a credit of `USD 4,000.00` against an open Accounts Receivable 1200 item of `USD 6,500.00`, which records a partial reconciliation of `USD 4,000.00` and leaves a residual of `USD 2,500.00`

### Scenario 4: An accepted settlement difference is written off in a balanced adjustment entry

- **Given** an additional seeded statement line on the same `US-01` **Bank** journal for `+USD 995.00`, unreconciled; and one open customer invoice of `US-01` on Accounts Receivable 1200 with a residual of `USD 1,000.00` for the same partner, so the shortfall between the two is exactly `USD 5.00`; and a write-off account list filtered to Bank Charges 6800 together with the accounts the group chart of accounts designates for reconciliation differences, so the destination of a difference is chosen from a governed list rather than from the whole chart — each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Chief Accountant reconciles that statement line against that invoice and directs the `USD 5.00` difference to Bank Charges 6800 as a write-off
- **Then** the invoice reaches a residual of `USD 0.00` and the statement line's reconciled state becomes true with its own residual at `USD 0.00`; the entry posts through the `US-01` **Bank** journal as debit Bank 1010 `USD 995.00` and debit Bank Charges 6800 `USD 5.00` against credit Accounts Receivable 1200 `USD 1,000.00`, so total debits of `USD 1,000.00` equal total credits of `USD 1,000.00` at a difference of `USD 0.00`, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01; the written-off amount is recorded as the exact figure `USD 5.00` rather than as a difference described in words, and the Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 line of the provisional posting clears to `USD 0.00`; and the same shape holds for the parent feature's worked line `L-0322`, a credit of `USD 9,995.00` in `US-01` against an open Accounts Receivable 1200 item of `USD 10,000.00`, which posts debit Bank 1010 `USD 9,995.00` and debit Bank Charges 6800 `USD 5.00` against credit Accounts Receivable 1200 `USD 10,000.00` for total debits of `USD 10,000.00` equal to total credits of `USD 10,000.00` at a difference of `USD 0.00`. A **whole journal entry created from inside the reconciliation**, for a line no existing journal item explains, is a distinct case from the write-off of a difference on a line that does have a counterpart — it carries the full statement-line amount rather than a difference — and it is a different trigger from this one: Criterion 8 owns it, together with the reversal link CF-003 requires

### Scenario 5: One reversal undoes the write-off reconciliation and restores the line, the invoice and the write-off account

- **Given** the write-off reconciliation of Criterion 4 on the `US-01` **Bank** journal, with the `+USD 995.00` statement line reconciled, its invoice at a residual of `USD 0.00` under a matching number and `USD 5.00` sitting in Bank Charges 6800, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Chief Accountant reverses that write-off reconciliation
- **Then** the statement line returns to a reconciled state of false with its move restored to the provisional form debit Bank 1010 `USD 995.00` against credit Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 `USD 995.00`, total debits of `USD 995.00` equal to total credits of `USD 995.00` at a difference of `USD 0.00`; the invoice returns to open at a residual of `USD 1,000.00` with the matching number withdrawn from both records; the `USD 5.00` write-off is undone by a balanced reversal entry, so the net movement on Bank Charges 6800 for that reconciliation is `USD 0.00`; where the reversed reconciliation had generated a whole journal entry of its own under Criterion 8, or an exchange-difference entry under Criterion 7, that entry is reversed with it rather than left stranded, by a reversal entry whose total debits equal its total credits at a difference of `0.00` in the company currency; and the reversal is recorded with the acting Chief Accountant, the timestamp and the reconciliation state before the change, so the count of reversals carrying no record of who performed them is 0 — with each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01. Reversing one allocation of an item that carries two is a different reversal and therefore a different trigger, whose case is the parent feature's worked line `L-0330`

### Scenario 6: An invalid reconciliation attempt is refused and nothing is written

- **Given** the unreconciled statement line of company `US-01` dated 2024-02-12, bank reference `UTL-0212`, narration "Utility Bill Payment", amount `−USD 425.75`, counterparty "City Power & Water", on the `US-01` **Bank** journal; the Bank 1010, Bank Charges 6800 and Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 balances of `US-01` together with the count of stored journal entries and partial reconciliation records, each recorded before the attempt; open journal items of `NL-01` **Global Europe SARL** within the same amount range; a journal-entries lock date of 2024-02-29 in force for `US-01`, which closes the period 2024-02-12 falls in; and the 2024-02-08 statement line `FEE-0208` "Monthly Bank Service Fee" of `−USD 35.00` on the same journal, which a reconciliation rule of `STORY-001-04-02` has already reconciled under a matching number — each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Chief Accountant confirms one of the reconciliation attempts of the Examples table below
- **Then** that attempt is rejected with a message naming its failing condition; the count of journal entries created is 0, the count of partial reconciliation records created is 0, and no entry whose total debits differ from its total credits is created at any point, so an unbalanced posting is refused at confirmation rather than saved and repaired afterwards; the reconciled state and the residual of the statement line the attempt targeted are unchanged from before it; the Bank 1010, Bank Charges 6800 and Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 balances of `US-01` equal the balances recorded before the attempt at a difference of `USD 0.00`, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01; and the message names the failing condition and the remedial action while disclosing no stack trace, no SQL, no file-system path and no credential

### Scenario 7: A foreign-currency part allocation records three currency amounts with its rate basis and its exchange difference

- **Given** an additional seeded statement line on the EUR **Bank** journal of company `NL-01` **Global Europe SARL**, whose functional currency is EUR, for `+EUR 900.00` with a transaction date of 2025-03-20; and one open customer invoice of `NL-01` on Accounts Receivable 1200 denominated `USD 1,000.00`, raised on 2025-03-01 when the published rate was 1.1000 USD per EUR and therefore carried in the books of `NL-01` at `EUR 909.09`; and the published closing rate held on `res.currency.rate` for 2025-03-20 of **1.0850 USD per EUR**, expressed as units of the foreign currency per unit of the company's functional currency — each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at its currency's `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Chief Accountant allocates that `EUR 900.00` statement line against that `USD 1,000.00` invoice as a part payment
- **Then** the partial reconciliation records three amounts rather than one: the company-currency amount `EUR 900.00`, the debit-side currency amount `EUR 900.00` on the Bank 1010 side and the credit-side currency amount `USD 976.50` on the Accounts Receivable 1200 side; the conversion applies the `res.currency.rate` effective on the statement line's own transaction date 2025-03-20 of 1.0850 USD per EUR rather than a rate of the allocation date or of the reporting date, and that rate together with its rate date is shown with the allocation so `EUR 900.00` at 1.0850 giving `USD 976.50` is recomputable after the fact; the settlement posts through the `NL-01` **Bank** journal as debit Bank 1010 `EUR 900.00` against credit Accounts Receivable 1200 `EUR 900.00`, total debits of `EUR 900.00` equal to total credits of `EUR 900.00` at a difference of `EUR 0.00`; the realized exchange difference the partial reconciliation points at posts as debit Accounts Receivable 1200 `EUR 12.27` against credit FX Gain/Loss on Settlement 7100 `EUR 12.27` — the difference between the `EUR 900.00` received and the `EUR 887.73` at which the settled `USD 976.50` portion was carried at the invoice-date rate of 1.1000 USD per EUR — so total debits of `EUR 12.27` equal total credits of `EUR 12.27` at a difference of `EUR 0.00`, which is the treatment IAS 21 requires of a settlement difference; and the invoice residual is restated in USD as `USD 23.50` to 2 decimal places, the invoice staying open. The clear-down of the period's last unexplained line and the report tie-out that follows it are a different trigger; Criterion 8 owns them.

### Scenario 8: A line no journal item explains is cleared by an entry created from inside the reconciliation and the period ties to nil

## Edge Cases

| # | Edge Case | Expected Handling |
|---|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | A statement line arrives with an amount of `USD 0.00` — a fee reversed within the same statement, or a zero-value advice the bank sends for information | The line is retained rather than discarded, because deleting it breaks the continuity between the bank's own record and the reconciled ledger that the **Bank Reconciliation Statement** relies on. It is reconciled without generating an unbalanced posting: where a `USD 0.00` line needs no movement, the count of journal entries created for it is 0 and its reconciled state is set to true directly; where it is reconciled against a journal item, the entry created carries total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `USD 0.00`. No `USD 0.00` allocation is written as a partial reconciliation record, because a partial reconciliation of nothing settles nothing, and the count of partial reconciliation records carrying an amount of `USD 0.00` is 0. Every amount in this row is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01 |
| 2 | A reconciliation, a write-off or a reversal is dated inside a period closed by a lock date — a reconciliation dated 2024-02-12 on the `US-01` **Bank** journal with a journal-entries lock date of 2024-02-29 in force for `US-01` | The attempt is blocked before any entry is built, with an Odoo validation message naming the journal-entries lock date 2024-02-29 and the affected company `US-01`; the count of journal entries created is 0, the count of partial reconciliation records created is 0, and the Bank 1010, Bank Charges 6800 and Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 balances of `US-01` are unchanged at a difference of `USD 0.00`, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01. The line keeps a reconciled state of false and stays visible on the **Bank Reconciliation Statement** for the same date range with its date, amount, label and age rather than being netted away. A reversal is bound by the same lock date as a posting, so a reconciliation made in an open period and reversed after the period closes is refused with the same named message rather than silently re-dated. Whether the platform refuses the posting outright or re-dates it into the first open period is established against the branch DEC-001 confirms and asserted as the one observed outcome, because the two differ in whether the Chief Accountant re-presents the line or looks for the movement in a later period; the lock date itself is administered by [`STORY-001-01-05`](../FEATURE-001-01/STORY-001-01-05-configure-period-lock-dates.md) |
| 3 | A foreign-currency allocation converts to an amount whose rounding leaves a residual below the minor unit of the item's currency — an allocation converting at 1.0850 USD per EUR on 2025-03-20 that settles an item to within less than `USD 0.01` of nil | The conversion states its rate basis — the `res.currency.rate` of the statement line's own transaction date, 1.0850 USD per EUR on 2025-03-20, stored with the allocation alongside its rate date — and the sub-unit remainder is absorbed into FX Gain/Loss on Settlement 7100 within the same reconciliation, so the item reaches a residual of exactly `USD 0.00` and no sub-unit residual is left stranded on an item that finance considers settled. The absorbing entry carries total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `EUR 0.00` in the company currency of the posting entity, and the absorbed amount is recorded as an exact figure rather than described. Every amount in this row is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at its currency's `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01, per the ISO 4217 minor unit held on `res.currency` |
| 4 | Two accountants confirm a reconciliation of the same statement line at the same time, each having opened the candidate list before the other confirmed | Exactly 1 reconciliation survives. The second confirmation is rejected with a message naming the statement line and the matching number the first confirmation assigned to it, so the second accountant reads which line was taken rather than a generic conflict; the count of journal entries created by the second attempt is 0, the count of partial reconciliation records created by it is 0, and the count of statement lines carrying two reconciliations is 0. The refused selection is not partially applied — no subset of its journal items is reconciled — because a half-applied selection would leave neither accountant able to state which items still need a decision. The Bank 1010 and Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 balances of the affected company after the refused attempt equal the balances after the first confirmation at a difference of `USD 0.00`, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01 |
| 5 | One invoice is settled by statement lines that fall in different fiscal periods — an Accounts Receivable 1200 item of `USD 1,000.00` of `US-01` receiving `USD 500.00` on 2025-03-28 and `USD 500.00` on 2025-04-02 | Each partial allocation keeps the date of the statement line that produced it, so the March allocation stays in March and the April allocation stays in April and neither is re-dated to make the pair sit in one period; the allocation history states both amounts, both dates and the latest matched date of 2025-04-02, which is the date the aged receivable presentation and the period tie-out read; the full reconciliation that completes the settlement is dated in the later period, 2025-04, because that is when the item became settled, and the item is reported as open at `USD 500.00` at the 2025-03-31 period end rather than retrospectively closed; and both allocations remain individually reversible, with reversing the March allocation moving the residual from `USD 0.00` back to `USD 500.00` while the April allocation stays intact and the full-reconciliation record and its matching number are withdrawn. Every amount in this row is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD `res.currency` rounding increment of 0.01 |

**Areas to analyse against this shape.** The matching-number assignment routine that walks the graph of partials to decide which lines form one settled set, and what it does to an assigned number when one partial leaves the graph. The unlink path that cascades from removing one partial: whether it reverses or deletes the exchange-difference entry the partial points at, whether it removes the covering full-reconciliation record, and how it recomputes the residual on both linked lines. And the constraint that requires the currency-amount fields to be populated, together with what it rejects, since a record carrying an allocation amount of `USD 0.00` is one Edge Case 1 asserts is never written.

### Audit Trail and Traceability

Every decision in this story is a judgement a person made, which is what distinguishes it from the rule-driven matching of `STORY-001-04-02`. The trail therefore records the decision, not only its result.

- Each reconciliation records the acting Chief Accountant, the timestamp, the statement line, the journal items selected, the amount allocated to each, and the destination and amount of any difference written off
- Each entry created from inside the reconciliation records the counterpart account, the journal, the company and the acting role, so a posting that exists only because a reconciler created it is never indistinguishable from one raised by a business document
- Each reversal records the acting Chief Accountant, the timestamp and the reconciliation state before the change — the residuals, the matching number and the postings as they stood — so the state a reversal returned to can be compared with the state it left
- Where a write-off exceeds the threshold agreed in Sub-Task 3, the second signature is recorded with the role that gave it and the amount it covered
- The trail is readable from the statement line to the journal item and back, and from the **Bank Reconciliation Statement** line to the statement line, the candidates considered against it and the entry finally posted, so the External Auditor tests the reconciliation control rather than re-performing the reconciliation

## Estimation

| Dimension | Rating | Basis |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| **Effort** | Medium to high | One workspace carrying six distinct operations that must interoperate: a filtered candidate search with deterministic ordering and stated response-time budgets, a full match against one item or many under a single matching number, a part allocation that leaves a stated residual and an allocation history, an accepted difference written off to a governed account inside the same entry, a whole entry created from inside the reconciliation for a line no item explains, and selective reversal of any one of them with its cascade — across 7 criteria each asserted numerically, 5 edge cases and 22 sub-tasks over four roles |
| **Complexity** | Medium to high | The hard part is not the match, it is what happens around it. A part allocation must leave an exact residual, withhold the matching number while the item stays open, and still be reversible on its own without disturbing the other allocations on the same item — which means the cascade from one removed partial has to reach the covering full-reconciliation record and the exchange-difference entry and stop there. A reversal must restore the statement line's provisional posting against Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 and undo a generated entry with a balanced reversal rather than a deletion, and it must be bounded by the same lock date as the posting it undoes. The foreign-currency allocation must select its rate on the line's own transaction date, record three currency amounts on one record, and post its `EUR 12.27` difference to FX Gain/Loss on Settlement 7100 in balance. And the confirmation boundary must be atomic against a second session reconciling the same line |
| **Uncertainty** | Low | The platform already carries the whole data model this story asserts against: the partial and full reconciliation records with their company-currency and currency-side amounts, the exchange-difference link, the matching-number routine that walks the graph of partials, the cascade on removing one partial, and the undo action that resets a statement line to its suspense counterpart. `account_bank_reconciliation_ce` 19.0.1.0.0 already extends the partial-reconciliation path and already carries the statement report. That removes most of the design risk, leaving the commissioned work as the filtered search with its budgets, the governed write-off destination, the CF-003 entry creation, the multi-company proof and the tie-out evidence. DEC-001 changes the field surface, not the expected values |
| **Story Points (Fibonacci)** | **8** | Medium-to-high effort and medium-to-high complexity against low uncertainty |

**Rationale.** The 8 points sit on four things that are delivered together because none of them can be demonstrated alone: a filtered candidate workspace whose ordering repeats and whose search returns inside a stated budget; a part allocation that leaves an exact residual and withholds settlement; a balanced write-off and a balanced entry created from inside the reconciliation; and selective reversal that undoes one decision, cascades to what that decision generated, and leaves every other allocation standing. It is one step below the 13 of `STORY-001-04-02` because there is no scoring model to design, no weighting to agree and no rule-expression surface to bound — the data model this story needs already exists and already behaves, so the work is a workspace, a governed destination list, a cascade proved in both directions and the evidence that the period ties out.

The two retired flat-layout stories that merge here were each sized "L (Large)" on a T-shirt scale. Those estimates are restated as one Fibonacci value of **8** on the scale the Epic mandates, with Effort, Complexity and Uncertainty rated separately above. The parent feature records that if refinement finds the combined scope above 13 points, that is raised as a backlog change against its four-story budget rather than absorbed silently or trimmed; at 8 points this story sits inside that budget with room, and the natural split line if one were ever needed is the exact match against the part allocation — which is deliberately not taken, because a workspace that can match a line exactly and cannot allocate part of it sends the reconciler back to a journal entry by hand for every payment that arrives short.

### Governance of This Story

### Migration From the Superseded Backlog

**Migration line.** `STORY-001-04-03` supersedes and replaces **two** retired flat-layout stories, which the Epic's retirement map records as merging here: `BR-003 Manual Reconciliation` and `BR-005 Partial Reconciliation`, whose retired identifiers stay reserved for the Epic's retirement-map traceability and are stated in this one line only — `STORY-001-04-03` is this story's sole identifier everywhere else.

From the first came the candidate search with its filters, the single and multi-item match, the matching number, the write-off taken during reconciliation, the whole entry created from inside the reconciliation, the undo, the audit trail of who decided what and when, the multi-currency display and the pagination and response-time expectations. From the second came the part allocation, one payment applied across many invoices, the part allocation combined with a write-off, the allocation history with its latest matched date and remaining balance, the selective un-reconciliation that leaves other allocations intact, the multi-currency partial with its exchange difference, and the shapes of the partial and full reconciliation records. Their domain content is carried forward and re-authored sentence by sentence, and five of their conventions are deliberately superseded: the generic "Accountant / Bookkeeper" persona becomes the named **Chief Accountant**; the two T-shirt estimates become one Fibonacci value of 8; the 3-to-6 criterion guidance becomes the 4-to-8 bound, here 7; the flat "Odoo 18.0" target becomes the Epic-inherited platform statement with the version mismatch flagged as an open decision; and the retired blanket prohibition on Enterprise dependencies is withdrawn at Epic level and replaced by the open edition decision. The merge is deliberately **not** reversible into two stories inside this backlog: part allocation is not planned as a separate future story, because a workspace that can match a line exactly and cannot allocate part of it sends the reconciler back to a journal entry by hand for every payment that arrives short.

### Business Context

This is the story that lets the reconciliation be finished rather than nearly finished. `STORY-001-04-02` resolves 950 of 1,000 lines and leaves 50, and those 50 are not the leftovers of a job all but done — they are the payment that arrived short, the receipt that covers three invoices, the charge no document was ever raised for, and the match somebody made last week that turned out to be wrong. None of those can be resolved by a rule, because each requires a judgement about the ledger: which account absorbs a difference, whether an item is part-settled or written off, and whether a posted decision should stand. That is why the persona is the Chief Accountant and not the Treasury Analyst who owns the rest of this feature.

The consequence of leaving the 50 unworked is not a smaller number on a dashboard. The **Bank Reconciliation Statement** cannot be signed while any line is unexplained, the Cash Flow Statement of `STORY-001-07-03` has no proved opening and closing cash, and the period close of `STORY-001-07-05` is gated on a bank reconciliation that is clear. A reconciliation that is 95% complete blocks the same amount of downstream work as one that is 0% complete.

### Why the Exact Match and the Part Match Belong Together

The two are the same decision taken against a different amount. The reconciler opens one line, searches the same candidates with the same filters, and selects the same kind of journal item; what differs is whether the amounts agree. If they do, the item closes and a matching number is assigned. If they do not, either the remainder stays open as a stated residual or the difference is directed to a named account. Splitting those into two stories would produce a workspace that handles the tidy case and hands the untidy one back to a manual journal entry, which is precisely the outcome the retired backlog produced and this one supersedes. Reversal is held with them for the same reason: a reconciliation nobody can undo is a reconciliation nobody will make, and a reconciler who cannot undo one allocation without undoing all of them will avoid part allocation altogether.

### Scope Boundary

This story searches candidates, matches in full and in part, accepts a difference, creates an entry from inside the reconciliation and reverses any of those decisions. It does not import a statement — that is [`STORY-001-04-01`](./STORY-001-04-01-import-bank-statements.md). It does not score a candidate, author or sequence a reconciliation rule, or preview a rule against historical lines — that is [`STORY-001-04-02`](./STORY-001-04-02-auto-match-statement-lines.md), and this story consumes the exception queue that one leaves rather than duplicating its engine. It does not open, count or close a cash register — that is [`STORY-001-04-04`](./STORY-001-04-04-manage-cash-registers.md). It defines no account and no journal: Bank 1010, Accounts Receivable 1200, Accounts Payable 2000 and Revenue 4000 come from the group base chart and Cash 1000, Suspense / Outstanding Payments 1099 and FX Gain/Loss on Settlement 7100 from the chart-extension registry, both owned by [FEATURE-001-01](../FEATURE-001-01-chart-of-accounts-fiscal-year.md). It defines no tax rate and no fiscal position: where a write-off line carries a tax, the rate is the one FEATURE-001-05 defines and this story asserts only that its base and tax amounts post separately and in balance. It does not build the **Bank Reconciliation Statement** — it asserts the value that report must read once the period is cleared.
