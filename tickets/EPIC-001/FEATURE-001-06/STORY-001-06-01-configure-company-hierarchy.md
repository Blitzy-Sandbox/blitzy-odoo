This is the root story of the `FEATURE-001-06/` folder. It configures, from the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)), the group whose books the other four stories of the feature consolidate — **Global Holdings Inc.** (entity code `US-01`, functional currency USD, which is also the group presentation currency), **Global Europe SARL** (`NL-01`, functional currency EUR, wholly owned at 100.00%, incorporated by the parent on 2019-01-01) and **Global Asia Pte Ltd** (`SG-01`, functional currency SGD, wholly owned at 100.00%, incorporated by the parent on 2021-01-01), which are the register's entities in existence at the 2025-03-31 worked reporting date; the register's fourth entity, **Global UK Ltd** (`GB-01`, functional currency GBP, wholly owned at 100.00%), is incorporated on 2025-04-01 and is configured on the identical attribute set when it enters the group — together with the closing, average and historical rates that translate each subsidiary into USD. Ordering rule **ORD-003** in the Epic places this story ahead of every other story in the feature for that reason: an intercompany entry has no second set of books to post into, and a consolidation rule has no entity chart to map, until the hierarchy and its currencies exist.

## User Story

**As a** Group Controller

**I want** the group's legal entities held as one consolidation scope in the system of record — Global Holdings Inc. as the consolidation parent of Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd, each of the three a company in its own right carrying its own functional currency, and each subsidiary carrying its ownership percentage and full consolidation as its method, with USD fixed as the group presentation currency and with the closing, average and historical rates that translate each subsidiary into it recorded against a rate date

**So that** every consolidation run reads the group's composition and its translation rates from governed configuration instead of from a workbook tab, and Global Europe SARL's Trial Balance total assets of `€850,000.00 EUR` reach the group presentation currency as `$922,250.00 USD` at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR — rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 — with no manual conversion step between the entity ledger and the group position (SM-013).

## Acceptance Criteria

Seven criteria are authored, inside the 4-to-8 bound the Epic sets for a story, and they carry the mandated coverage distribution: **Scenarios 1, 2 and 3** are the valid-input cases (the euro-area subsidiary, the Singapore subsidiary, then the rate that translates one of them); **Scenario 4** is the invalid and incomplete-input case; **Scenario 5** is the error-handling case; **Scenario 6** is the accounting edge case, where a locked period refuses a functional-currency change; and **Scenario 7** is the multi-company isolation case required by C-014 and D-007.

Every criterion names the company whose configuration or books are affected. Every amount states its currency and its rounding, and every translated amount also states the rate and the rate date it was translated at, so no figure below is a bare conversion. The scenarios are cumulative: Scenario 1 leaves the group at two entities, Scenario 2 brings it to three, and Scenarios 3 to 7 are asserted against that three-entity group.

### How the group is held: a consolidation scope beside the companies, not a company hierarchy

The criteria below are written against a **consolidation-scope contract** rather than against Odoo's company parent-child mechanism, because that mechanism cannot express this group. Three facts read in this repository at Odoo 19.0 Community decide it, and each is re-verified against the platform target DEC-001 confirms:

- **A parent link cannot be changed after a company exists.** `res.company.write()` refuses any `parent_id` value outright with `The company hierarchy cannot be changed.` (`odoo/addons/base/models/res_company.py` L341–L343). A parent may be set only when the company record is created, so no story can re-parent Global Europe SARL or Global Asia Pte Ltd onto Global Holdings Inc. after the fact.
- **A child of a parent company cannot hold its own currency.** `currency_id` is the one field `_get_company_root_delegated_field_names()` delegates from the root (L104), the `parent_id` onchange copies it down (L184–L189), and the constraint `_check_root_delegated_fields` (L407–L414) raises `The Currency of a subsidiary must be the same as it's root company.` on create and on write alike. A EUR entity and an SGD entity therefore **cannot** sit beneath a USD parent through `parent_id` at all.
- **The mechanism is named for what it models.** The reciprocal of `parent_id` is `child_ids`, labelled **"Branches"** (L37), with `_parent_store = True` and a stored `parent_path` (L21, L39). It models branches of one legal entity measured in one currency — not separate legal entities each with its own functional currency.

Accordingly: **each of the three entities is a top-level `res.company` record holding no parent company and its own functional currency**, and the group's composition — consolidation parent, ownership percentage, consolidation method, group-membership flag and the date from which membership applies — is held on a **consolidation-scope record beside the company**, which references `res.company` rather than duplicating it (C-012). Where a genuine Odoo branch is required later — a branch of Global Europe SARL measured in EUR, inside the same legal entity — it is created with its parent set at creation time and inherits EUR from its root; a branch is never used to express a subsidiary. Whether the scope record is a dedicated model or an extension carrying these attributes on the company is settled by the implementing agent in § Technical Discovery Notes; what the criteria fix is that the relationship is a consolidation relationship, that no entity in the group carries an Odoo parent company, and that each entity keeps its own functional currency.

### Scenario 1: Euro-area subsidiary admitted to the consolidation scope with its own functional currency and ownership

- **Given** Global Holdings Inc. (entity code `US-01`) exists as a company whose functional currency is USD and which holds no parent company, USD is fixed as the group presentation currency, and Global Europe SARL (entity code `NL-01`) exists as a company whose functional currency is EUR and which likewise holds no parent company, carrying no consolidation-scope membership, no ownership percentage and no consolidation method, while the group chart of accounts and its IFRS and US GAAP taxonomy released by FEATURE-001-01 are present in both companies
- **When** the Group Controller saves the consolidation-scope membership of Global Europe SARL in one operation: consolidation parent Global Holdings Inc., ownership percentage 100.00%, full consolidation as its method, and group membership effective from its incorporation date of 2019-01-01
- **Then** the consolidation scope presents Global Holdings Inc. as the consolidation parent of Global Europe SARL and Global Europe SARL as a consolidated subsidiary of it, not as a branch of it; the functional currency of Global Europe SARL reads EUR and that of Global Holdings Inc. reads USD, each held on its own company record; the count of group members whose company record holds a parent company is 0, which is what allows EUR to sit beside USD at all; the ownership percentage of Global Europe SARL reads 100.00% and its consolidation method reads full consolidation; the group presentation currency held for the group stays USD; across Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL the count of members with no consolidation parent where one is required, no functional currency, or no ownership percentage is 0; and no posted journal item in either company is altered by the change, so the Trial Balance of Global Europe SARL for the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 reports total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `€0.00 EUR`, rounded to 2 decimal places at the EUR rounding increment of 0.01

### Scenario 2: Singapore subsidiary admitted in a third currency with its ownership percentage and method

- **Given** Global Holdings Inc. is the consolidation parent of Global Europe SARL at functional currency EUR and ownership percentage 100.00% as configured in Scenario 1, and Global Asia Pte Ltd (entity code `SG-01`) exists as a company whose functional currency is SGD, holding no parent company, no consolidation-scope membership, no ownership percentage and no consolidation method
- **When** the Group Controller saves the consolidation-scope membership of Global Asia Pte Ltd in one operation: consolidation parent Global Holdings Inc., ownership percentage 100.00%, full consolidation as its method, and group membership effective from its incorporation date of 2021-01-01
- **Then** the consolidation scope presents Global Holdings Inc. as the consolidation parent of both Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd, the functional currency of Global Asia Pte Ltd reads SGD, its ownership percentage 100.00% and its consolidation method full consolidation; the count of companies in the consolidation scope of Global Holdings Inc. is 3 including Global Holdings Inc. itself — the scope as constituted at the 2025-03-31 worked reporting date, before Global UK Ltd (`GB-01`) is incorporated on 2025-04-01 and consolidated from the quarter beginning that date ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)) — and the count of consolidated subsidiaries is 2, while the count of those 3 companies holding an Odoo parent company remains 0 so that USD, EUR and SGD each stay the functional currency of their own entity; because Global Asia Pte Ltd is wholly owned, the non-controlling interest arising on it is 0.00% and no minority-interest measurement is carried for it, matching the Epic's scope boundary; the entity codes `US-01`, `NL-01` and `SG-01` each resolve to exactly one company, so no two entities share an identifier; the group presentation currency stays USD; and the group composition — consolidation parent, functional currency, ownership percentage, consolidation method, membership date and country of incorporation per entity — is readable as configuration for the IFRS 12 disclosure rather than held in narrative notes

### Scenario 3: Closing rate recorded against its rate type and the euro-area Trial Balance reaches the group presentation currency

- **Given** the three-entity consolidation scope of Scenario 2 is in force with USD as the group presentation currency; the consolidation-rate table already carries the EUR average rate of 1.0720 USD/EUR valid for the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31, the EUR historical rate of 1.1000 USD/EUR as of 2019-01-01 for the incorporation of Global Europe SARL, the SGD closing rate of 0.7450 USD/SGD as of 2025-03-31, the SGD average rate of 0.7380 USD/SGD valid for the same date range and the SGD historical rate of 0.7300 USD/SGD as of 2021-01-01, each record carrying its currency pair, its rate type, its validity — an as-of date for a closing or historical rate and a date range for an average rate — and its source; no EUR closing rate is recorded as of 2025-03-31; and the Trial Balance of Global Europe SARL for the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 reports total assets of `€850,000.00 EUR` with total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `€0.00 EUR`, every amount rounded to 2 decimal places at the EUR rounding increment of 0.01
- **When** the Group Controller records the EUR closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR as of the rate date 2025-03-31
- **Then** the total assets of `€850,000.00 EUR` held by Global Europe SARL translate to `$922,250.00 USD` at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01, and that translated total is reproducible from the stored rate rather than from a figure typed by hand; **all three EUR rate classes coexist and each is addressable by rate type together with its validity** — closing 1.0850 USD/EUR as of 2025-03-31, average 1.0720 USD/EUR valid for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 and historical 1.1000 USD/EUR as of 2019-01-01 — so a consolidation run selects a rate by type and not by date alone and no translation reads an undated or untyped rate; the closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR as of 2025-03-31 does not displace the average rate of 1.0720 USD/EUR that covers the same date, and the count of EUR rates readable for 2025-03-31 by their three rate types is 3, which is the assertion that distinguishes this contract from the daily rate table of `res.currency.rate`, where `unique (name,currency_id,company_id)` admits one rate per date per currency per company and no rate type exists to tell two of them apart (`odoo/addons/base/models/res_currency.py` L375–L378); the three SGD rates for Global Asia Pte Ltd stay unchanged at 0.7450 USD/SGD closing as of 2025-03-31, 0.7380 USD/SGD average for the same date range and 0.7300 USD/SGD historical as of 2021-01-01; the count of currency pairs and rate types required by the group and missing from the consolidation-rate table falls to 0; and the decimal precision applied to each translated amount is read from the currency record of the group presentation currency USD, which carries a rounding increment of 0.01 and therefore 2 decimal places

### Scenario 4: Consolidation-scope membership saved with no ownership percentage and no group rate pair is refused

- **Given** the three-entity consolidation scope of Scenario 2 is in force, holding Global Holdings Inc., Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd; a candidate fourth company **Global Mexico S. de R.L.** (`MX-01`) exists as a `res.company` record carrying functional currency MXN — a company cannot exist without a currency, because `currency_id` on `res.company` is required (`odoo/addons/base/models/res_company.py` L52), which is why the attributes tested here are the ones the **consolidation-scope membership** carries rather than the ones the company record already guarantees; and the consolidation-rate table holds no MXN currency pair at any rate type
- **When** the Group Controller saves the consolidation-scope membership of `MX-01` with its ownership percentage left empty and its consolidation method left unselected
- **Then** the save is refused with a validation message that names the ownership percentage and the consolidation method as the required membership attributes that are absent, and names MXN as a currency pair the consolidation-rate table holds at no rate type; no consolidation-scope membership is created for `MX-01`, so the consolidation scope still holds exactly the 3 companies Global Holdings Inc., Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd and the count of consolidated subsidiaries stays at 2; no consolidation-rate record is written for MXN and the count of MXN rate records stays at 0; Global Europe SARL keeps functional currency EUR at ownership 100.00% and Global Asia Pte Ltd keeps functional currency SGD at ownership 100.00%; and the translated total assets of Global Europe SARL as of 2025-03-31 stay at `$922,250.00 USD` at the closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01

### Scenario 5: Circular consolidation scope is refused and the group structure is left intact

- **Given** the three-entity consolidation scope of Scenario 2 is in force, with Global Holdings Inc. as the consolidation parent of both Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd, and no company record in the group holding an Odoo parent company
- **When** the Group Controller sets the consolidation parent of Global Holdings Inc. to Global Europe SARL, which already sits beneath it in the same scope
- **Then** the save is refused with a validation message that names Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL and states that the assignment would place an entity inside its own consolidation scope; the scope is unchanged, so Global Holdings Inc. remains the consolidation parent of both Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd and holds no consolidation parent of its own; Global Europe SARL keeps functional currency EUR at ownership 100.00% and Global Asia Pte Ltd keeps functional currency SGD at ownership 100.00%; the count of companies in the consolidation scope of Global Holdings Inc. stays at 3 including itself and the count of members holding an Odoo parent company stays at 0; and no consolidation-rate record, no configuration attribute of another entity and no posted journal item in any of the three companies is modified by the refused attempt

### Scenario 6: Functional-currency change refused for a company whose period is locked

- **Given** Global Europe SARL carries posted journal items for the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 whose Trial Balance reports total assets of `€850,000.00 EUR` with total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `€0.00 EUR`, each amount rounded to 2 decimal places at the EUR rounding increment of 0.01, its journal-entry lock date is set to 2025-03-31 under the closing controls owned by STORY-001-01-05, and the EUR closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR is recorded as of the rate date 2025-03-31
- **When** the Group Controller changes the functional currency of Global Europe SARL from EUR to USD
- **Then** the change is refused with a validation message that names Global Europe SARL and its journal-entry lock date of 2025-03-31 — a guard **this story delivers**, because the currency field on the company record carries no lock-date check in the platform baseline and would otherwise accept the write; Global Europe SARL keeps EUR as its functional currency and 100.00% as its ownership percentage; every journal item already posted in Global Europe SARL keeps the EUR amount it was posted at, so its Trial Balance for the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 still reports total assets of `€850,000.00 EUR` with total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `€0.00 EUR`, rounded to 2 decimal places at the EUR rounding increment of 0.01; the translated total assets of Global Europe SARL as of 2025-03-31 stay at `$922,250.00 USD` at the closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01, so the group position already published for that period stays reproducible; and the message states the re-measurement path — a change of functional currency applies from a date after the lock date, with the entity's comparative balances re-presented rather than its locked journal items rewritten

### Scenario 7: Company isolation holds across the three entities

- **Given** the three-entity consolidation scope of Scenario 2 is in force; a Consolidation Accountant whose allowed companies contain Global Europe SARL alone; posted journal items for the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 in all three companies; and a Group Controller whose allowed companies contain all three, whose own read of the same range has already been performed and returned the journal items of all 3 companies with the Trial Balance total assets of Global Europe SARL at `€850,000.00 EUR` and those of Global Asia Pte Ltd at `S$1,200,000.00 SGD`, each rounded to 2 decimal places at the rounding increment of 0.01 for its currency
- **When** the Consolidation Accountant reads the group's journal items for the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31
- **Then** the result contains the journal items of Global Europe SARL alone, no journal item of Global Asia Pte Ltd and no journal item of Global Holdings Inc. is returned, and the count of companies readable by the Consolidation Accountant is 1 — consolidation-scope membership grants no implicit read of a sibling entity, and because no member holds an Odoo parent company a `parent_of` record-rule domain resolves to the member itself rather than widening to the group; the Group Controller result seeded in the Given is unchanged at 3 companies, so the restricted read narrowed the population rather than the group read widening it; and the cross-company read performed on behalf of the group is recorded with the persona and the companies it covered, so the External Auditor reads which entities were accessed and by whom rather than inferring it

## Edge Cases

| # | Edge Case | Expected Handling |
|---|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Zero or absent value in a required group attribute** — an entity declared a consolidated subsidiary of Global Holdings Inc. is saved with its ownership percentage set to 0.00%, or with its functional currency left empty | Both saves are refused with an Odoo validation message naming the attribute at fault: an ownership percentage of 0.00% states that the parent holds nothing, which contradicts the declaration of the entity as a consolidated subsidiary, and a company with no functional currency has no unit to measure its books in. No company record is created and no attribute of an existing entity is changed, so the group stays at 3 companies with Global Europe SARL at functional currency EUR and ownership 100.00% and Global Asia Pte Ltd at functional currency SGD and ownership 100.00%. An entity held for sale and excluded from the group is expressed by clearing its group-membership flag, not by setting its ownership percentage to 0.00% |
| 2 | **Fiscal-period lock already covers the change** — the functional currency of Global Europe SARL, or the consolidation-scope membership that places it under Global Holdings Inc., is changed after journal items have been posted in a period on or before its journal-entry lock date of 2025-03-31 | The change is refused with an Odoo validation message naming Global Europe SARL and the lock date of 2025-03-31, and every posted journal item keeps the EUR amount it was posted at, so the translated total assets of Global Europe SARL as of 2025-03-31 stay at `$922,250.00 USD` at the closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01, and the group position already published for that period stays reproducible. A change of group structure takes effect from a date after the lock date and is recorded as a dated change to the consolidation-scope membership, and where the group has to reopen the period it does so through the recorded lock-date exception path rather than by editing locked journal items. The Odoo company parent link is not a route around this: it cannot be changed after the company exists at all (`odoo/addons/base/models/res_company.py` L341–L343) |
| 3 | **An entity whose currency rounding increment is not 0.01** — a fourth entity measured in a currency such as JPY, whose rounding increment is 1 and whose decimal places are therefore 0, joins the group whose presentation currency USD carries a rounding increment of 0.01 and 2 decimal places | Precision is read from each currency record at translation time rather than assumed: the entity's own balances are held and presented at the decimal places its functional currency declares, and the amount translated into the group presentation currency USD is rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01. The rounding difference that arises on translation is presented in Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 rather than absorbed into Retained Earnings 3100, and no code path hard-wires 2 decimal places for an entity currency |
| 4 | **Entity joins the group part-way through the fiscal year** — an entity acquired on 2025-02-15 inside the reporting date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 | The acquisition date is recorded against the entity alongside its ownership percentage, and only results arising from 2025-02-15 onward belong to the group, while its pre-acquisition reserves are carried at the historical rate on the acquisition date rather than at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of the reporting period. | 5 | **Consolidation parent that would place an entity inside its own scope** — the consolidation parent of Global Holdings Inc. is set to Global Europe SARL or to Global Asia Pte Ltd, or an entity is set as its own consolidation parent | The save is refused with a validation message naming both entities in the attempted link, the existing scope is left intact with Global Holdings Inc. as the consolidation parent of both Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd, and the count of companies in the consolidation scope of Global Holdings Inc. stays at 3 including itself. A genuine restructuring — an intermediate holding company inserted between Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL — is expressed on the scope records and not on the company records: the new entity is created as a top-level company with its own functional currency and admitted to the scope under Global Holdings Inc., then the consolidation parent on Global Europe SARL's membership record is repointed to it with the effective date recorded, so no intermediate state is circular and no company record is re-parented — which the platform would refuse outright (`odoo/addons/base/models/res_company.py` L341–L343) |

### Multi-Company Access and Untrusted Input

- [ ] **C-014 and D-007 — Company isolation is respected rather than bypassed.** Record rules and the allowed-companies mechanism scope which entities a finance role reads; the cross-company read a consolidation run performs is an explicit, logged elevation rather than a suspension of record rules, and Scenario 7 is the test that proves it.
- [ ] **C-015 through C-022 — The exchange-rate feed is an untrusted boundary.** The feed that supplies the closing, average and historical rates this story records is validated before a rate record is written: response content type and size are checked against an allowlist, XML is parsed with document-type definitions and external-entity resolution disabled and entity expansion bounded, the payload is validated against its declared schema before any field is read, and each rate value is range-checked. A rejected response writes no rate record, creates no journal entry, leaves the last confirmed rate in force, discloses no stack trace or complete payload, leaves the service available, and leaves 0 temporary artifacts behind (CWE-459). C-022 requires this story to carry at least one acceptance test per hostile case.
- [ ] **Rate-source authenticity, freshness and revision control (CWE-345 — insufficient verification of data authenticity).** A well-formed, schema-valid response is **not** evidence that the rate is genuine, current or new, and a translated group position is only as trustworthy as the rate behind it. Five controls therefore apply on top of the parsing validation above. **Trusted source:** the endpoint is an allowlisted provider named in configuration, reached over TLS with certificate-chain and hostname verification and a pinned certificate authority, with credentials held in configuration and never in a ticket, a fixture or source. **Authenticity:** the payload's signature or message authentication code is verified against the provider's published key before any field is read, so a forged but well-formed response is refused. **Freshness:** the payload carries a publication timestamp and a sequence or version, and a rate whose publication timestamp falls outside the staleness window the group's rate policy states for its rate date is refused as stale rather than stored. **Replay:** a provider, currency pair, rate date and sequence tuple already seen is refused as a replay, and the last confirmed rate stays in force. **Governed revision after a lock:** where the provider publishes a revised rate for a rate date whose period is locked and whose consolidated pack has been issued, the revision does not overwrite the stored rate — it is held pending, requires the Group Controller's approval with the Chief Accountant informed, and is stored as a new dated revision that retains the superseded rate and its own approval record, so a pack already published stays reproducible. Each of the five carries its own test in § Test Requirements.

### Accounting Standards Compliance

- [ ] **IFRS 10 and US GAAP ASC 810 — consolidation scope.** Control determines the scope: Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd are wholly owned at 100.00% and are consolidated in full, which is why no non-controlling interest arises and no minority-interest measurement is configured. Non-controlling-interest allocation and equity-method accounting are outside this Epic's scope.
- [ ] **IAS 21 and US GAAP ASC 830 — functional currency and translation.** Each entity's functional currency is recorded as a governed attribute, and the rate classes this story configures carry the translation policy the sibling stories apply: closing rate for assets and liabilities at the as-of date, average rate for income and expense over the reporting date range, and historical rate for subscribed capital at the incorporation date, with the residual presented in Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 rather than in Retained Earnings 3100.
- [ ] **IFRS 12 — disclosure of interests in other entities.** The composition of the group — parent, ownership percentage, functional currency and country of incorporation per entity — is held as reportable configuration, so the disclosure is read from the system of record.

| **Uncertainty** | Medium-Low | The uncertainty is named rather than open: where the new ownership attributes live is a bounded design question, and the direction a stored rate expresses is answered by reading the currency-rate model. Two programme decisions sit above the story — DEC-001 for the platform version and DEC-002 for the edition source — but neither changes the outcome, because every model this story configures is present under all three candidate versions and under every candidate edition path |
| **Story Points** | **5** | Fibonacci scale (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) |

**Why 5 and not 3.** The configuration surface is small and well understood, which alone would place the story at 3. Two factors move it to 5: the ownership percentage, the consolidation method and the group-membership flag are new configuration with no field to reuse, so a design decision precedes the build; and the verification breadth is wider than the build, because the record-rule interaction across three entities, the circular-hierarchy guard and the locked-period refusal each need a test of their own on top of the seven acceptance tests.

**Why 5 and not 8.** Nothing in the story waits on an unanswered question outside it, no data migration is involved, no report is produced, and no journal entry is posted by it. The intercompany postings, the consolidation rule set, the eliminations and the consolidated statements — the parts that carry the feature's real arithmetic — are each a separate sibling story.

### Integration Test Considerations

- [ ] Test the group against `res.company` for all three entities, asserting that each is top-level — no parent company, and `root_id` resolving to the company itself — that each holds its own functional currency, and that the consolidation scope resolves Global Holdings Inc. as the consolidation parent of the other two. - [ ] Test typed-rate retrieval for each of the six consolidation rates — 3 for EUR and 3 for SGD — asserting that all three classes of one currency pair coexist, that retrieval by rate type returns the intended one where two share a date, and that the stored rate direction is asserted before any translated amount.
- [ ] Test translation of Global Europe SARL's Trial Balance for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 into the group presentation currency USD, asserting `$922,250.00 USD` from `€850,000.00 EUR` at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01.
- [ ] Test record-rule scoping end to end through `res.users` and `ir.rule` with two personas and three companies, and assert the audit record the cross-company read leaves behind.
- [ ] Test the lock-date interaction against the per-company lock dates in `account`, including the recorded exception path for a period that has to be reopened.
- [ ] Test that `account.account` and `account.journal` records resolve per company for all three entities before a cross-entity posting is attempted.
- [ ] **Hostile-input tests on the exchange-rate feed path (C-022)** — one per case, each a **rejection**: a malformed response, a schema-invalid response, a payload carrying an external-entity reference, an oversized response, a disallowed content type and an out-of-range rate value. Each asserts a named rejection error, that no rate record is written on either the consolidation-rate structure or `res.currency.rate`, that no journal entry is created, that the last confirmed rate stays in force so the translated total assets of Global Europe SARL as of 2025-03-31 stay at `$922,250.00 USD` at the closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR, that the service stays available, and that 0 temporary artifacts remain (CWE-459).
- [ ] **Rate-source authenticity, freshness, replay and post-lock revision tests (CWE-345)** — five cases, each with one outcome. A response that is well-formed and schema-valid but whose signature fails verification against the provider's published key is **refused as unauthenticated**. A response served from an endpoint outside the allowlist, or over a connection whose certificate chain or hostname fails verification, is **refused before any field is read**. A payload whose publication timestamp falls outside the staleness window stated for its rate date is **refused as stale**. A payload repeating a provider, currency pair, rate date and sequence tuple already stored is **refused as a replay**. A provider revision of the EUR closing rate for 2025-03-31, whose period is locked and whose consolidated pack has been issued, is **held pending rather than applied**: the stored rate stays 1.0850 USD/EUR, the translated total stays `$922,250.00 USD` rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01, and only the Group Controller's recorded approval creates a new dated revision that retains the superseded rate. All five assert a structured audit record naming the provider, the currency pair, the rate date and the outcome, that no journal entry is created, and that the service stays available.

## Demonstration Path

The story is accepted when the **Group Controller** walks the **Finance Controller** and the **Product Owner** through the path below in the Odoo user interface, with the Chief Accountant present to confirm the chart and journal set of each entity and the Treasury Analyst present to confirm the rate source. The walkthrough is recorded against this story.

1. **Settings ▸ Users & Companies ▸ Companies** — the three companies shown as three top-level records, each opened to show its own functional currency (USD for Global Holdings Inc. `US-01`, EUR for Global Europe SARL `NL-01`, SGD for Global Asia Pte Ltd `SG-01`) and **no parent company on any of them**; then the consolidation scope opened beside them, showing Global Holdings Inc. as the consolidation parent of the other two, an ownership percentage of 100.00% and full consolidation as the method for each subsidiary, the membership dates of 2019-01-01 and 2021-01-01, and USD as the group presentation currency. The reviewers are shown why the two views are separate: a parent company on a member would force its currency to the parent's, and could not be removed afterwards.
2. **The live admission step** — Global Asia Pte Ltd admitted to the consolidation scope in one operation, showing the scope resolve so that Global Holdings Inc. becomes the consolidation parent of both subsidiaries, the count of consolidated subsidiaries reads 2, Global Asia Pte Ltd keeps SGD, and the count of members holding an Odoo parent company still reads 0.
3. **Accounting ▸ Configuration ▸ Currencies**, opening EUR and SGD to show the rounding increment of 0.01 and the resulting 2 decimal places, then the typed consolidation-rate list showing the closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR as of 2025-03-31 **beside** the average rate of 1.0720 USD/EUR valid for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 and the historical rate of 1.1000 USD/EUR as of 2019-01-01 — three EUR rates readable for the same period, each by its own rate type — and the SGD rates of 0.7450, 0.7380 and 0.7300 with their own types and validity, each row showing its source and the timestamp the rate was published at.
4. **The Trial Balance of Global Europe SARL for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31** — total assets of `€850,000.00 EUR` with total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `€0.00 EUR`, followed by the same total presented in the group presentation currency at `$922,250.00 USD`, translated at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR and rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01.
5. **The negative walkthrough** — a candidate subsidiary admitted with no functional currency and no ownership percentage and refused; the consolidation parent of Global Holdings Inc. set to Global Europe SARL and refused as circular; a functional-currency change on Global Europe SARL refused against its journal-entry lock date of 2025-03-31; and a provider revision of the 2025-03-31 EUR closing rate held pending rather than applied, the stored rate still reading 1.0850 USD/EUR — with the company count shown unchanged at 3 and the translated total still `$922,250.00 USD` after all four attempts.
6. **The isolation walkthrough** — the same journal-item read performed twice, once as a Consolidation Accountant holding Global Europe SARL alone, returning that company's items only, and once as the Group Controller holding all three companies, returning all three entities' items, with the readable company counts of 1 and 3 shown.

**API alternative.** Where a reviewer prefers the public API, the same six steps are demonstrated over JSON-RPC or XML-RPC against the `/jsonrpc` and `/xmlrpc/2/object` endpoints: reading the three top-level companies and their functional currencies on `res.company` and reading and writing the consolidation-scope memberships beside them, reading `res.currency` for the rounding increment and decimal places, writing and reading the typed consolidation rates by currency pair, rate type and validity, reading the translated Trial Balance total for Global Europe SARL, attempting each of the three refused operations and capturing the validation message returned as a fault, and repeating the journal-item read under two sessions whose allowed companies differ so the record-rule scoping is observed from outside the interface.

## Notes

### Business Context

Group reporting in this organization is a spreadsheet exercise because the group itself is not held anywhere. No parent-subsidiary relationship, no ownership percentage and no group presentation currency exist against Global Holdings Inc., Global Europe SARL or Global Asia Pte Ltd, so a consolidation run has nothing to read and an entity added mid-year reaches the group statements only when someone extends a formula range. This story is the smallest change that turns the group's composition into governed configuration, and it is the reason the four sibling stories can assert amounts at all: an intercompany entry needs two configured sets of books, a consolidation rule needs an entity chart and a rate class, an elimination needs a matched pair, and a consolidated statement needs a presentation currency.

### Why the Ownership Percentage Is Recorded Even at 100.00%

Both subsidiaries are wholly owned, so the ownership percentage changes no arithmetic today. It is recorded for three reasons. It is the IFRS 12 disclosure of the group's composition, which is reportable rather than incidental. It is the attribute a future acquisition of a partly owned entity would be measured against, so the group structure does not have to be re-modelled when one arrives. And it makes the consolidation scope explicit: an entity with no ownership percentage is an entity nobody has decided about, which is exactly the state Scenario 4 refuses.

### Open Questions for Refinement

- Which attribute expresses that an entity is in the consolidation scope but dormant for a period — the group-membership flag alone, or a dated scope record — and how a dormant entity is presented on the group statements.
- Whether the group presentation currency belongs on the parent company record or on a group-structure record, which decides what happens if the group ever reports in a currency no entity uses as its functional currency.
- Whether an intermediate holding company is expected between Global Holdings Inc. and either subsidiary, which would deepen the consolidation scope beyond two levels and change what a group read has to traverse; the company records themselves stay flat either way, since a parent link cannot be added to an existing company.
- Which rate source is authoritative per currency pair, and which provider signing key and staleness window the group's rate policy names for each. The handling of a revised rate published after a period has been locked and the pack issued is **not** open: it is held pending the Group Controller's approval and stored as a new dated revision that retains the superseded rate, under the CWE-345 controls in § Constraints.
