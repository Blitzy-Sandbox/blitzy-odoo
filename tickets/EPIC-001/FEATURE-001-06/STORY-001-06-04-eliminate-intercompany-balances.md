This is the fourth story of the `FEATURE-001-06/` folder and the one where the group stops owing money to itself. It **executes** what its two predecessors produced: the intercompany population [STORY-001-06-02](./STORY-001-06-02-post-intercompany-transactions.md) posted in two companies' books, and the elimination registry [STORY-001-06-03](./STORY-001-06-03-define-consolidation-rules.md) registered as Rule 4 of the consolidation rule set. Three eliminations are posted for the quarter ended 2025-03-31, each as one entry through the **group Miscellaneous journal** and each carrying total debits equal to total credits: the **intercompany balance** between Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL, the **intercompany trading** between the same two companies, and the parent's **investment** against each subsidiary's subscribed capital. Two obligations run through every criterion below. Every elimination entry states both of its totals, because an elimination that does not balance is a defect the group discovers in its own Balance Sheet. And Intercompany Receivable 1300 and Intercompany Payable 2100 must net to `$0.00 USD` at group level, with any residual **reported on the Intercompany Reconciliation report rather than absorbed** — no plug entry, no rounding into another account. Rendering the consolidated statements is the work of [STORY-001-06-05](./STORY-001-06-05-generate-consolidated-financials.md); this story posts the eliminations those statements present the result of, and retains the elimination working the External Auditor reads instead of re-performing ([Epic Definition of Done item 3](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#13-epic-level-definition-of-done)). Every entity name, functional currency, ownership percentage, account code and rate below is consumed unchanged from the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)) and its canonical group chart of accounts ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)); this story defines no entity, introduces no account code and creates no rate.

## User Story

**As a** Consolidation Accountant

**I want** to execute the three registered eliminations for a reporting period — the intercompany balance between Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL, the intercompany trading between those same two companies, and Global Holdings Inc.'s Investment in Subsidiary 1700 against the subscribed Share Capital 3000 of Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd — each posted through the group Miscellaneous journal as one entry whose total debits equal its total credits, with every unmatched residual reported on the **Intercompany Reconciliation** report and the whole elimination working retained per run

**So that** the group presents what it earned from and owes to the outside world instead of what it owes itself: Intercompany Receivable 1300 and Intercompany Payable 2100 each net to `$0.00 USD` at group level, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 and with the arithmetic residual before rounding inside the `$0.02 USD` tolerance SM-012 states; the `$40,000.00 USD` shared-services charge from Global Holdings Inc. to Global Europe SARL contributes `$0.00 USD` to consolidated revenue and `$0.00 USD` to consolidated cost; Investment in Subsidiary 1700 of `$256,000.00 USD` is removed against the same amount of subsidiary Share Capital 3000 so group equity counts the subscription once; and the External Auditor reads a retained working that names the company behind every eliminated amount rather than re-performing the consolidation (SM-012, SM-013, Epic Definition of Done item 3).

## Acceptance Criteria

Eight criteria are authored, at the upper bound of the 4-to-8 range the Epic sets for a story, and they carry the mandated coverage distribution: **Scenarios 1, 2 and 3** are the valid-input cases, one per elimination class (balance, trading, investment); **Scenario 4** is the invalid and incomplete-input case, where one side of a pair has not been recorded; **Scenario 5** is the error-handling case, where the period is locked; **Scenario 6** is the accounting edge case, where the eliminated amount is measured on the balance rather than on the ownership percentage; **Scenario 7** covers the zero-amount pair; and **Scenario 8** covers a second run started against a period already being eliminated.

Three authoring rules apply to every criterion below, and they are what make an elimination provable rather than assertable. **Every elimination criterion states both totals** — total debits and total credits — because an elimination whose legs disagree is a defect the group reads back in its own Balance Sheet. **Every criterion names the company or companies whose balances the elimination removes** by registered name: Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`), Global Europe SARL (`NL-01`) and Global Asia Pte Ltd (`SG-01`), never "the subsidiary" and never "the other entity". And **every monetary figure states its currency, its amount and its rounding**, with every translated figure also stating the rate and the rate date it was translated at, so no figure below is a bare conversion. Rounding is half-up to the decimal places implied by each currency's rounding increment, read from the currency record rather than assumed: 2 decimal places at the increment of 0.01 for USD, EUR and SGD.

### The three eliminations this story posts

The eliminations are stated in full before the criteria assert them, so each criterion asserts part of a registry that is written down rather than a figure invented at run time. All three are drawn unchanged from Rule 4 of the rule set registered by [STORY-001-06-03](./STORY-001-06-03-define-consolidation-rules.md) and from the parent Feature's elimination map.

| # | Elimination | Debit | Credit | Amount | Companies whose balances are removed | Basis |
|---|-------------|-------|--------|--------|--------------------------------------|-------|
| 1 | Intercompany balance | Intercompany Payable 2100 | Intercompany Receivable 1300 | `$40,000.00 USD` | Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL | The matched intercompany pair, the payable leg of `€36,866.36 EUR` restated at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR |
| 2 | Intercompany trading | Revenue 4000 | Expense 6100 | `$40,000.00 USD` | Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL | The shared-services charge raised in one entity and borne by the other, removed from group revenue and group cost at one amount |
| 3 | Investment against subsidiary equity | Share Capital 3000 | Investment in Subsidiary 1700 | `$256,000.00 USD` = `$110,000.00 USD` for Global Europe SARL + `$146,000.00 USD` for Global Asia Pte Ltd | Global Holdings Inc. against Global Europe SARL and against Global Asia Pte Ltd | The parent's carrying amount against each subsidiary's subscribed capital, both at the historical rate of that subsidiary's incorporation date |

Four facts about these three entries decide the criteria, and each is stated once here rather than repeated in prose below.

- **The journal is the group Miscellaneous journal.** Every elimination entry posts through the **Miscellaneous** journal reserved for consolidation entries, which [STORY-001-06-02](./STORY-001-06-02-post-intercompany-transactions.md) holds back for this story: the Sales journal of Global Holdings Inc. and the Purchase journal of Global Europe SARL carry the intercompany charge, and Global Europe SARL's **own** Miscellaneous journal carried the `€170.68 EUR` retranslation of its USD-denominated payable to Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss 7200. None of those three journals carries an elimination. Which books hold it is **settled, not deferred**: the group Miscellaneous journal belongs to `GRP-01` (**Global Group Consolidation**, functional currency USD), the consolidation-ledger company of the Epic’s canonical legal-entity register [Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register). That follows from the platform rather than from preference — a journal belongs to one company and a journal entry belongs to one company, so three eliminations are three entries only if one company owns the journal, and that company cannot be a statutory entity without its own ledger carrying group-only entries. **Move count: 3** — one `account.move` per elimination, each dated 2025-03-31, each with `company_id` = `GRP-01`, each balanced on its own. **Statutory isolation:** no entry is created in `US-01`, `NL-01` or `SG-01` by any elimination, so each statutory ledger and each single-entity statement is byte-for-byte what it was before the run.
- **The investment elimination is the subscribed capital and nothing else.** Investment in Subsidiary 1700 in Global Holdings Inc.'s books stands at `$256,000.00 USD`: `$110,000.00 USD` for Global Europe SARL, being `€100,000.00 EUR` at the 2019-01-01 historical rate of 1.1000 USD/EUR, plus `$146,000.00 USD` for Global Asia Pte Ltd, being `S$200,000.00 SGD` at the 2021-01-01 historical rate of 0.7300 USD/SGD. Each subsidiary was incorporated by the parent at subscription value and is **wholly owned at 100.00%**, so the elimination measures a non-controlling interest of 0.00%, recognizes no goodwill, and carries **one credit leg** with no minority-interest leg (IFRS 10, ASC 810; [Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)).
- **The residual rule is absolute.** Intercompany Receivable 1300 and Intercompany Payable 2100 must net to `$0.00 USD` at group level, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01, with the arithmetic residual before rounding — `$0.0006 USD` on the worked pair — inside the `$0.02 USD` tolerance SM-012 states. **Any residual beyond that is reported on the Intercompany Reconciliation report and is never absorbed:** no plug entry is written, and no residual is rounded into Retained Earnings 3100, Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 or Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss 7200.
- **The entity ledgers are not rewritten.** An elimination is an **additional** entry at group level, so no journal item posted by STORY-001-06-02 is altered or reversed by a run: Global Holdings Inc. still carries Intercompany Receivable 1300 of `$40,000.00 USD` and Global Europe SARL still carries Intercompany Payable 2100 of `€36,866.36 EUR` in their own books, which is what allows every eliminated amount to be traced back to the journal items of the named company that produced it.

### Scenario 1: Intercompany balance between Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL eliminated to a group balance of zero

- **Given** the consolidation rule set registered by [STORY-001-06-03](./STORY-001-06-03-define-consolidation-rules.md) is in force for the reporting as-of date 2025-03-31 and holds the balance-elimination pair of Intercompany Payable 2100 against Intercompany Receivable 1300 with the group Miscellaneous journal named on it; the population posted by [STORY-001-06-02](./STORY-001-06-02-post-intercompany-transactions.md) stands, so Global Holdings Inc. carries Intercompany Receivable 1300 of `$40,000.00 USD` against Global Europe SARL and Global Europe SARL carries the mirrored Intercompany Payable 2100 of `€36,866.36 EUR`, which restates at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR to `$40,000.00 USD`, each amount rounded to 2 decimal places at the rounding increment of 0.01 for its currency; the **Intercompany Reconciliation** report as of 2025-03-31 presents that pair matched at an unmatched residual of `$0.00 USD`; the fiscal period ending 2025-03-31 is open in Global Holdings Inc., Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd; and no elimination entry exists for the period
- **When** the Consolidation Accountant runs the balance elimination for the period ending 2025-03-31
- **Then** one entry dated 2025-03-31 in the group Miscellaneous journal debits Intercompany Payable 2100 `$40,000.00 USD`, the balance originating in **Global Europe SARL's** books, and credits Intercompany Receivable 1300 `$40,000.00 USD`, the balance originating in **Global Holdings Inc.'s** books, so **total debits of `$40,000.00 USD` equal total credits of `$40,000.00 USD` at a difference of `0.00 USD`**, each amount rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01; after the entry, Intercompany Receivable 1300 nets to `$0.00 USD` and Intercompany Payable 2100 nets to `$0.00 USD` at group level across Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL, the arithmetic residual before rounding of `$0.0006 USD` staying inside the `$0.02 USD` tolerance SM-012 states; that group net of `$0.00 USD` is the balance a later statement reads — presenting it on the **Consolidated Balance Sheet** is the work of [STORY-001-06-05](./STORY-001-06-05-generate-consolidated-financials.md) and is asserted there, because no statement is run by this posting; no journal item posted by STORY-001-06-02 is altered or reversed, so Global Holdings Inc. still carries `$40,000.00 USD` at Intercompany Receivable 1300 and Global Europe SARL still carries `€36,866.36 EUR` at Intercompany Payable 2100 in their own books and both eliminated amounts trace back to those journal items; and no residual is written to Retained Earnings 3100, Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 or Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss 7200 by the run

- **When** the Consolidation Accountant runs the trading elimination for the period ending 2025-03-31

### Scenario 3: Investment in Subsidiary eliminated against the subscribed capital of both subsidiaries

### Scenario 6: The eliminated amount is measured on the balance rather than on the ownership percentage

- **Given** the Epic's canonical legal-entity register records Global Europe SARL at an ownership percentage of **100.00%** and Global Asia Pte Ltd at an ownership percentage of **100.00%**, each fully consolidated with a non-controlling interest measured at 0.00% and no goodwill; the group's stated elimination policy under IFRS 10 and ASC 810 measures an elimination on the **balance** rather than on the holding; the matched pair stands at Intercompany Receivable 1300 of `$40,000.00 USD` in Global Holdings Inc.'s books against Intercompany Payable 2100 of `€36,866.36 EUR` in Global Europe SARL's books, restated at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR to `$40,000.00 USD`, each amount rounded to 2 decimal places at the rounding increment of 0.01 for its currency; and the fiscal period ending 2025-03-31 is open in both companies

### The Re-Run Contract

A period is eliminated more than once in practice — an entity balance is corrected after the first run — so the behaviour is fixed here as **supersede by version**, in one atomic transaction, rather than left as a choice between reversing and replacing:

| Property | Contract |
|----------|----------|
| Identity | Every elimination run for a period produces a **numbered, dated version** of the set. Version 1 is the first run for the period ending 2025-03-31 |
| Atomicity | A re-run reverses the 3 entries of the version in force and posts the 3 entries of the next version **in a single transaction**. A failure part-way leaves 0 new entries and 0 reversals, and the version in force is unchanged |
| In-force count | The count of elimination entries **in force** for a period is always 3 — one per elimination — whatever the version number. After one deliberate re-run the period holds 3 in force (version 2), 3 superseded (version 1) and 3 reversals, so the net group effect of the superseded version is `$0.00 USD` |
| Never in place | No entry of a superseded version is edited, unposted or deleted; it is reversed. That is what keeps an issued pack reproducible from the version it was issued from |
| Evidence | The retained working names the version in force, the version it superseded, the run parameters and the corrected entity balance that prompted the re-run, so the External Auditor reads why the set changed rather than inferring it |
| Reporting | A statement run for the period reads the version **in force** at the reporting date. A pack already issued from version 1 stays reproducible because version 1 and its reversals are both retained |

### Accounting Standards Compliance

- [ ] **IFRS 10 and US GAAP ASC 810 — elimination in full.** Intercompany balances, intercompany revenue and intercompany cost are eliminated **in full**, and the parent's investment is eliminated against the subsidiary's equity. The eliminated amount is measured on the balance and is never scaled by the ownership percentage: 100% of an intercompany balance is removed at any holding, and an interest held outside the group is presented as a separate component of group equity rather than by eliminating a share of the balance. In this group both subsidiaries are wholly owned at 100.00%, so the non-controlling interest measured is 0.00%, no goodwill arises, and the investment elimination of `$256,000.00 USD` is the subscribed capital and nothing else. **Non-controlling-interest allocation and equity-method accounting are outside the Epic's scope** ([§8.1](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#81-explicitly-excluded-items)), which is why this story presents no such equity component and **introduces no account code**: were a partly owned entity ever admitted to the Epic's canonical legal-entity register, the equity code for its non-controlling interest would be claimed through the [FEATURE-001-01 §7.4 Group Chart-of-Accounts Extension Registry](../FEATURE-001-01-chart-of-accounts-fiscal-year.md#74-group-chart-of-accounts-extension-registry) and added to [Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts) in the same change, before any criterion cited it.
- [ ] **IAS 21 and US GAAP ASC 830 — the rate behind each eliminated leg.** A translated leg is eliminated at the rate class and rate date the rule set names, not at a rate chosen by the run: the intercompany payable of `€36,866.36 EUR` at the 2025-03-31 closing rate of 1.0850 USD/EUR, and each investment component at the historical rate of its subsidiary's incorporation date — 1.1000 USD/EUR as of 2019-01-01 and 0.7300 USD/SGD as of 2021-01-01. The retranslation of a monetary balance stays a separate event in the entity's own books at Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss 7200, and the translation residual stays in Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 at `$11,090.00 USD`; an elimination writes to neither.
- [ ] **IFRS 12 — disclosure of interests in other entities.** The retained elimination working, naming the company behind every eliminated amount and the ownership percentage of each subsidiary, is part of the evidence behind the group's composition disclosure, so it is read from the system of record rather than reconstructed from working papers.

### Discovery versus Prescription

**Fixed by this story and not open to redesign:** the three eliminations with their debit and credit accounts, their amounts and the group Miscellaneous journal; that every elimination entry carries total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `0.00 USD`, with both totals stated; that Intercompany Receivable 1300 and Intercompany Payable 2100 net to `$0.00 USD` at group level with any residual reported on the **Intercompany Reconciliation** report and never absorbed; that the eliminated amount is measured on the balance rather than on the ownership percentage; that a locked period, an unmatched pair, a zero-amount pair and a second concurrent run each post nothing and leave nothing behind; that no journal item posted by [STORY-001-06-02](./STORY-001-06-02-post-intercompany-transactions.md) is altered or reversed; the retention of the elimination working per run with the company named against every eliminated amount; and the Definition of Done below, including the 80% coverage gate and demonstrability in the Odoo user interface or through the public API.

**Deferred to agent discovery:** how the run-once guard is enforced under concurrency; where the per-pair residual is stored for the report to read; how the cross-company aggregation is authorized and logged; and the field-level and view-level design of every model this story touches. **Not deferred, because the platform or a canonical register decides it:** that the eliminations post in the group Miscellaneous journal of `GRP-01` as 3 entries, one per elimination, leaving the statutory ledgers of `US-01`, `NL-01` and `SG-01` untouched; that a re-run supersedes by version rather than reversing in place; that a journal entry belongs to one company and is refused unless it balances; that the group relationship is held on a consolidation-scope record rather than on `res.company.parent_id`; that this story introduces no account code, because a code is claimed in the FEATURE-001-01 §7.4 registry and the Epic's canonical chart before any criterion cites it; and that the entity names, functional currencies, ownership percentages, account codes and rates are those of the Epic's canonical registers.

## Estimation

| Dimension | Rating | Justification |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **Effort** | High | Three distinct elimination classes executed in one run, each with its own amount basis: a matched pair whose two sides are held in two companies and two currencies, a trading pair that must leave the group result unmoved, and an investment elimination composed of two subsidiary components measured at two historical rates on two different dates. On top of the generation sit the run's guards — an unmatched pair, a locked period, a zero-amount pair and a second concurrent run — the per-pair residual detection the **Intercompany Reconciliation** report reads, and the retained working with a company name against every eliminated amount |
| **Complexity** | High | The proof is harder than the posting. Each entry must balance **and** the group balance it produces must be `$0.00 USD` — two assertions that can disagree, since an entry balancing at `$40,000.00 USD` on both legs still leaves a group residual if the pair was aggregated at the wrong rate. The cross-entity assertion spans two sets of books, two functional currencies and a rate applied at full precision with rounding taken once at the presentation increment, so the `$0.0006 USD` arithmetic residual has to land inside the `$0.02 USD` tolerance rather than in an account. Run-once semantics under concurrency, atomicity so a failed run leaves 0 entries, and the residual rule that forbids a plug are each a behaviour to build rather than a check to add. The measurement rule — an elimination read from the balance and never scaled by the ownership percentage — is the single most common consolidation error and is asserted as its own criterion |
| **Uncertainty** | Low to medium | The unknowns are named and few: how the run-once guard is enforced under concurrency, and where the per-pair residual is stored for the report to read. The ledger owner, the move count and the re-run contract are settled rather than open. The entities, rates, account codes and elimination pairs all arrive from predecessors and are consumed unchanged. Two programme decisions sit above the story — DEC-001 for the platform version and DEC-002 for the edition source — and neither changes the elimination **content**, which is why the story is authorable now; DEC-002 changes only where the run is expressed |
| **Story Points** | **13** | Fibonacci scale (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) |

**Why 13 and not 8.** [STORY-001-06-03](./STORY-001-06-03-define-consolidation-rules.md) is an 8: it **specifies** three elimination pairs and proves each is specified to balance, while posting nothing and rendering nothing. This story posts. Every figure it asserts has to survive a real entry against real balances in three companies — three elimination classes rather than one, a cross-entity balance assertion that spans two currencies, a residual rule that forbids the one shortcut every manual consolidation takes, run-once semantics under concurrency with atomicity on failure, and a retained working that has to name the company behind each eliminated amount for the External Auditor. The verification surface is wider than the build: eight acceptance tests, the numeric assertions C-009 requires, and the group-level net-to-zero identity tested separately from the per-entry balance check.

**Why 13 and not 21.** Nothing in the story waits on an unanswered question outside it, no data migration is involved, and no statement is rendered by it — the consolidated pack belongs to [STORY-001-06-05](./STORY-001-06-05-generate-consolidated-financials.md). The pairs, the amounts, the accounts, the journal and the rates are all supplied by predecessors and consumed unchanged, the platform already refuses an unbalanced entry, and the three eliminations are 3 entries carrying 6 journal items rather than a general-purpose consolidation engine.

## Notes

### Business Context

Before elimination, the group's own numbers describe a company trading with itself. The `$40,000.00 USD` shared-services charge from Global Holdings Inc. to Global Europe SARL is revenue in one ledger and cost in the other, so added together it overstates group revenue and group cost by the same figure and every margin, covenant and incentive computed on that revenue is computed on a number that includes the group paying itself. The same charge stands as Intercompany Receivable 1300 in one company and Intercompany Payable 2100 in the other, so group assets and group liabilities are each overstated by `$40,000.00 USD`. And the `$256,000.00 USD` the parent carries at Investment in Subsidiary 1700 is the same subscription the subsidiaries carry at Share Capital 3000, so group equity counts it twice. This story is the entry that removes all three, and — more importantly — the proof that each removal balanced and that the accounts it targeted actually reached `$0.00 USD` at group level. That proof is what turns the elimination from a spreadsheet nobody keeps into evidence the External Auditor reads instead of re-performing.

### The Residual Rule, Stated Without Qualification

Intercompany Receivable 1300 and Intercompany Payable 2100 must net to `$0.00 USD` at group level, rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01. A sub-cent arithmetic residual arising from translating the EUR-side payable — `$0.0006 USD` on the worked pair — sits inside the `$0.02 USD` tolerance SM-012 states and presents as `$0.00 USD` after rounding. **Any residual is reported on the Intercompany Reconciliation report rather than absorbed silently.** There is no plug entry, no clearing of a difference to Retained Earnings 3100, no rounding into Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 or Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss 7200, and no netting of one pair's residual against another's. A residual outside the tolerance blocks the period and names both companies and its own amount, because a difference between two group companies is a bookkeeping fact one of them has to resolve — by recording the missing counterpart or by an agreed write-off in the owning company's books — and not a rounding problem for the consolidation run to close. This is the one rule in the story with no exception path.

### Elimination in Full, Whatever the Holding

The eliminated amount is measured on the **balance** and never on the ownership percentage. Under IFRS 10 and ASC 810, 100% of an intercompany balance is eliminated no matter what share of the subsidiary the parent holds, and an interest held outside the group is presented as a separate component of group equity — never by eliminating only the parent's share of the balance. Eliminating a fraction is the single most common consolidation error, which is why Scenario 6 asserts it as a criterion rather than leaving it to policy: the count of elimination entries carrying a fraction of a pair balance is 0. In this group the point is asserted at a holding of 100.00%, where the arithmetic of the correct and the incorrect treatment coincides, so the criterion asserts the **measurement rule** — the eliminated amount equals the pair balance and is not the product of the balance and the ownership percentage — rather than a difference in amount. Were a partly owned entity admitted to the Epic's canonical legal-entity register later, the elimination would stay at 100% of the balance and the equity code for the non-controlling interest would be claimed in the [FEATURE-001-01 §7.4 Group Chart-of-Accounts Extension Registry](../FEATURE-001-01-chart-of-accounts-fiscal-year.md#74-group-chart-of-accounts-extension-registry) and added to [Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts) in the same change, before any criterion cited it.

### Reconciliation With the Canonical Registers

An earlier authoring pass of this folder read Global Asia Pte Ltd as 80% owned with a 20% non-controlling interest presented at an equity code of its own, and an investment elimination measured on those figures. That reading is **superseded** by the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)), which records every subsidiary as wholly owned at 100.00% and states that no goodwill and no non-controlling interest arise, by the canonical group chart of accounts ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)), which carries no equity code for a non-controlling interest, and by [§8.1](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#81-explicitly-excluded-items), which places non-controlling-interest allocation outside the Epic's scope. The register's precedence rule is explicit — on any conflict between a ticket and the appendix, the appendix governs and the ticket is corrected — so this story consumes the register unchanged: an ownership percentage of 100.00% against each subsidiary, a non-controlling interest of 0.00%, an investment elimination of `$256,000.00 USD` that is the subscribed capital and nothing else, historical rates of 1.1000 USD/EUR as of 2019-01-01 and 0.7300 USD/SGD as of 2021-01-01, and **no new account code**. The same figures are stated by the parent Feature's elimination map and by Rule 4 of [STORY-001-06-03](./STORY-001-06-03-define-consolidation-rules.md), so the three files agree line for line. The accounting point the superseded reading was reaching for — that elimination is measured in full whatever the holding — is preserved as Scenario 6 and in the note above, which is where it belongs.

### Open Questions for Refinement

- The concurrency mechanism behind the run-once guard — an advisory lock, a state field on a run record, or a database constraint — is a discovery outcome. The **behaviour** is not: a second concurrent run posts nothing and reports the period as in progress, and a deliberate re-run supersedes by version per [The Re-Run Contract](#the-re-run-contract).
- Whether the group Miscellaneous journal sits in a dedicated consolidation company or in the consolidating entity Global Holdings Inc., which decides whether an elimination is ever refused by the lock date of an operating company rather than by the group's own.
- How long the retained elimination working is kept, and whether the External Auditor expects to read the working of a superseded run as well as the one in force.
- Whether an unmatched pair blocks the whole period, as Scenario 4 asserts, or whether the group ever wants a partial elimination set posted with the unmatched pair listed — the safer reading is recorded here, and a change to it is approved by the Group Controller rather than assumed by the run.
