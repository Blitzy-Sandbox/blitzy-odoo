## User Story

**As a** Financial Reporting Manager

**I want** to render the group's two statements for a reporting period from the closed and locked books of Global Holdings Inc., Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd — the **Consolidated Balance Sheet** for an as-of date and the **Consolidated Profit & Loss** for a date range, both in the group presentation currency USD, each translated at the rate class and rate date the registered translation policy names, each net of the posted eliminations, and each accompanied by the **Intercompany Reconciliation** report that states any residual — with the Consolidated Balance Sheet balancing at total assets equal to total liabilities plus equity and every presented line resolving to the entity journal items behind it

**So that** the group answers a lender, an investor, a regulator and its own board from the system of record within **48 hours of the last of the three entities applying its period lock date**, rather than after the **5 or more business days** a spreadsheet consolidation takes (SM-013): the Consolidated Balance Sheet as of 2025-03-31 presents total assets of `$3,520,250.00 USD` equal to total liabilities of `$1,398,000.00 USD` plus total equity of `$2,122,250.00 USD` at a difference of `0.00 USD`, every amount rounded to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01; the Consolidated Profit & Loss for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 presents Revenue 4000 of `$1,723,520.00 USD`, Expense 6100 of `$1,500,160.00 USD` and profit for the period of `$223,360.00 USD`, which ties to the profit line inside consolidated equity at a difference of `0.00 USD`; the `$40,000.00 USD` intercompany charge between Global Holdings Inc. and Global Europe SARL contributes `$0.00 USD` to each; the translation reserve is presented as its own equity component at Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 of `$11,090.00 USD` instead of being buried in retained earnings; and the External Auditor tests the group position by drilling from a consolidated line into the named company's journal items rather than by re-performing the consolidation (SM-012, SM-013, [Epic Definition of Done item 3](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#13-epic-level-definition-of-done)).

## Acceptance Criteria

Seven criteria are authored, inside the 4-to-8 bound the Epic sets for a story, and they carry the mandated coverage distribution: **Scenarios 1, 2 and 3** are the valid-input cases, one per presented outcome (the Consolidated Profit & Loss, the Consolidated Balance Sheet, and the translation reserve as its own equity component); **Scenario 4** is the structural-integrity case, where the Consolidated Balance Sheet balances and both statements tie back to the entity sub-ledgers; **Scenario 5** is the invalid and incomplete-input case, where a subsidiary still holds unposted entries; **Scenario 6** is the error-handling case, where a rate class the policy requires cannot be resolved; and **Scenario 7** is the accounting edge case, where an entity's membership of the group begins outside the reported range.

Four authoring rules apply to every criterion below, and they are what make a consolidated statement provable rather than assertable. **Every criterion names the report, its date parameter and at least one expected line value**, because a statement asserted without a figure is not a criterion. **Every criterion that spans entities names the affected company** by registered name — Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`), Global Europe SARL (`NL-01`), Global Asia Pte Ltd (`SG-01`) and Global UK Ltd (`GB-01`) — never "the subsidiary" and never "the other entity". **Every monetary figure states its currency, its amount and its rounding, and every translated figure also states the rate class, the rate and the rate date it was translated at**, so no figure below is a bare conversion. And **the Consolidated Balance Sheet is asserted to balance**, with total assets equal to total liabilities plus total equity and the group-specific equity component Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 inside equity; where an elimination entry is cited, both of its totals and every participating company are stated, because a statement built on an elimination that does not balance is wrong in a way the reader cannot see. Rounding is half-up to the decimal places implied by each currency's rounding increment, read from the currency record rather than assumed: 2 decimal places at the increment of 0.01 for USD, EUR and SGD.

**Where this story reads from, and what it does not do.** This story **posts nothing**. It reads the posted `account.move.line` records of the three companies in the consolidation scope, translates each class of balance at the rate class the registered policy names, overlays the three elimination entries [STORY-001-06-04](./STORY-001-06-04-eliminate-intercompany-balances.md) posted, and presents the result. No entity ledger is altered, no elimination is generated here, no rate record is written, and no account code is introduced. The single-entity Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss, their comparative columns, the statement-line drill-down convention and the PDF and XLSX export path are owned by [FEATURE-001-07](../FEATURE-001-07-financial-reporting-period-close.md) under its export and drill-down convention and are inherited unchanged; the criteria below assert the **consolidated** view and the group-specific lines only.

Three facts about these statements decide the criteria and are stated once here rather than repeated below.

- **The reports of this story are the group reports, and their names are distinct.** The **Consolidated Balance Sheet** takes an as-of-date parameter, the **Consolidated Profit & Loss** takes a date-range parameter, and the **Intercompany Reconciliation** report takes an as-of-date parameter and carries the residual disclosure. The single-entity **Balance Sheet** of [STORY-001-07-01](../FEATURE-001-07/STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md) and the single-entity **Profit & Loss Statement** of [STORY-001-07-02](../FEATURE-001-07/STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md) are separate reports run for one named company, and no criterion below asserts a figure of theirs.
- **A rate is selected by rate class and rate date, never by date alone.** Three EUR classes are readable for a reporting date — closing as of the reporting date, average for the reporting date range, and historical as of the entity's incorporation date — so selecting on date alone would return one of them arbitrarily. Opening Retained Earnings 3100 is carried at the USD amount the prior period's translated statements already presented and is not retranslated, which is what keeps an issued statement reproducible.
- **The eliminations are an overlay, not a rewrite.** Each entity's own books still carry the balances the eliminations remove at group level: Global Holdings Inc. still reads `$40,000.00 USD` at Intercompany Receivable 1300 and `$256,000.00 USD` at Investment in Subsidiary 1700, Global Europe SARL still reads `€36,866.36 EUR` at Intercompany Payable 2100, and Global Asia Pte Ltd still reads `S$200,000.00 SGD` at Share Capital 3000, each rounded to 2 decimal places at the rounding increment of 0.01 for its currency. That is what allows every consolidated line to be traced back to a named company's journal items.

## Constraints

### License and Compliance

- [ ] **C-001 — AGPL-3.0 compatible licence.** Any module delivering the consolidated aggregation, the translation application, the elimination overlay, the two statements or the Intercompany Reconciliation presentation is distributed under an AGPL-3.0 compatible licence, matching the Community-edition accounting add-ons already present in this repository.
- [ ] **C-002 — Existing licence respected.** `account.move`, `account.move.line` and `account.account` are `account` models and `res.company`, `res.currency` and `res.currency.rate` are `base` models, all under LGPL-3; any extension of them stays licence-compatible and no derived work misstates the licence of the code it extends.
- [ ] **C-005 and C-006 — Odoo and OCA standards.** Python follows Odoo and OCA module guidelines including PEP 8, and static analysis passes with the repository's configured tooling, whose lint configuration is declared in `ruff.toml` at the repository root. A defect in a consolidated statement reaches lenders, investors and regulators directly, so it is caught before review rather than after issue.
- [ ] **C-009 — Numeric accounting assertions.** Every figure this story presents is tested as an amount rather than inspected: the balance identity at a difference of `0.00 USD`, the report-to-report profit identity at a difference of `0.00 USD`, the aggregate-to-consolidated reconciliation on Revenue 4000 and Expense 6100, and the per-entity translation reconciliations for Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd are asserted values in automated tests.
- [ ] **C-012 — Build on the existing models.** Both statements are reads of `account.move.line` records classified through `account.account`, aggregated across the companies of the consolidation scope and overlaid with the elimination entries already posted. No parallel ledger, no shadow chart and no second copy of an entity balance is created, so a consolidated line and an entity Trial Balance read from the same journal items.
- [ ] **C-019 — Data access discipline.** Balance aggregation across companies, rate resolution, elimination overlay and residual detection are expressed through the Odoo ORM or parameterized SQL, with no string-concatenated query construction, because the reporting date, the as-of date, the company set and the account codes carry externally supplied parameter values into queries.
- [ ] **C-020 and C-021 — Output neutralization and no embedded credentials.** Values written to a CSV or XLSX export of either statement or of the Intercompany Reconciliation report are neutralized against formula injection, a refusal message discloses no stack trace and no complete payload, and no credential, key or certificate is present in source, logs, fixtures or exports.

DEC-002 is owned by the CFO / Finance Director with the Group Controller, the Epic gates FEATURE-001-06 through FEATURE-001-09 on its confirmation, and this story records the decision rather than pre-empting it ([Appendix B](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#appendix-b-open-decisions-register)).
- [ ] **The prior prohibition and the prior scope boundary are withdrawn, not restated.** The superseded flat-layout backlog that preceded this tree took the opposite position on every count that matters to this story: its own out-of-scope table declared **multi-company consolidation with intercompany eliminations** an excluded feature, its boundary table fixed **single-company operation** as the programme's company structure with multi-company consolidation held outside it, and it prohibited any dependency on Odoo Enterprise modules outright. This story is the direct refutation of that position, and the position is **superseded** rather than repeated — multi-entity financial operation is the stated objective of this Epic, which is why a consolidated statement exists at all; multi-company consolidation is in scope, with only non-controlling-interest allocation, equity-method accounting and goodwill measurement held out ([§8.1](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#81-explicitly-excluded-items)); and the blanket prohibition is replaced by the open edition decision above, because the absence of a module from this repository is a fact to plan around rather than a rule forbidding its use. The absolute "No Enterprise Dependencies" checkbox carried by the story template is superseded on the same ground.
- [ ] **C-004 — OCA ecosystem compatibility.** Whichever path DEC-002 confirms, the consolidated aggregation, the translated balances and the retained run stay consumable by Odoo Community Association add-ons, so an add-on reporting engine can present the group position without the consolidation being restated inside it.
- [ ] **The report engine is a discovery decision, not a constraint this story resolves.** [D-004](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#94-d-004-report-engine-and-drill-down-decision) holds the choice between extending the platform's own reporting infrastructure and adopting a dedicated engine, together with the drill-down and export path and the generation-time budget. This story states **what** each statement presents and **which** identities it must satisfy; it prescribes no engine.

### Multi-Company Access and Untrusted Input

- [ ] **C-014 — Company isolation is respected rather than bypassed.** Reading and aggregating the balances of Global Holdings Inc., Global Europe SARL and Global Asia Pte Ltd in one presentation is an explicit, logged elevation rather than a suspension of record rules, and the run executes only for a persona holding all three companies among its allowed companies. A Financial Reporting Manager scoped to Global Europe SARL alone can neither read nor present the journal items of Global Asia Pte Ltd or Global Holdings Inc. through this run, and the elevation records the persona and the companies it covered for the External Auditor ([D-007](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#97-d-007-multi-company-security-and-record-rules)).
- [ ] **C-015 through C-022 — This story consumes validated rates and carries no rate-feed ingestion.** Every rate behind a presented line — the closing rates of 1.0850 USD/EUR and 0.7450 USD/SGD as of 2025-03-31, the average rates of 1.0720 USD/EUR and 0.7380 USD/SGD for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31, and the historical rates of 1.1000 USD/EUR as of 2019-01-01 and 0.7300 USD/SGD as of 2021-01-01 — is resolved from a record that has already passed the trusted-source, transport, authenticity, freshness and replay controls C-015 through C-022 and CWE-345 require. Ingestion of the exchange-rate feed is configured by [STORY-001-06-01](./STORY-001-06-01-configure-company-hierarchy.md), and C-022 assigns the per-hostile-case acceptance tests on that path to that story and to [STORY-001-06-03](./STORY-001-06-03-define-consolidation-rules.md). What this story is accountable for is that an unresolvable rate class produces **no** statement rather than a partially translated one, and that a provider revision of a rate whose period is locked and whose pack has been issued never silently restates a published figure.
- [ ] **Report parameters are externally supplied values.** The as-of date, the reporting date range, the presentation currency and the entity scope arrive from outside the report and are validated before they reach a query: a date that resolves to no fiscal period, a currency that is not the group presentation currency of the scope, and an entity that is not a member of the scope at the requested date are each refused with a message naming the parameter, and none of them is interpolated into a query as text (C-019).

### Accounting Standards Compliance

- [ ] **IFRS 10 and US GAAP ASC 810 — consolidated presentation.** The group presents one set of statements covering the parent and the entities it controls, with intercompany balances, revenue and cost eliminated in full and the parent's investment eliminated against the subsidiary's equity, so the Consolidated Balance Sheet as of 2025-03-31 presents Intercompany Receivable 1300, Intercompany Payable 2100 and Investment in Subsidiary 1700 at `$0.00 USD` and Share Capital 3000 at the parent's `$500,000.00 USD`. Both subsidiaries are wholly owned at 100.00%, so the non-controlling interest measured is 0.00%, no goodwill arises, and the count of equity components presented for an interest held outside the group is 0. **Non-controlling-interest allocation, equity-method accounting and goodwill measurement are outside the Epic's scope** ([§8.1](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#81-explicitly-excluded-items)), which is why no such component is presented and this story **introduces no account code**: were a partly owned entity ever admitted to the Epic's canonical legal-entity register, the equity code for its non-controlling interest would be claimed through the [FEATURE-001-01 §7.4 Group Chart-of-Accounts Extension Registry](../FEATURE-001-01-chart-of-accounts-fiscal-year.md#74-group-chart-of-accounts-extension-registry) and added to [Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts) in the same change, before any criterion cited it.
- [ ] **IAS 21 and US GAAP ASC 830 — translation to the presentation currency.** Assets and liabilities are translated at the closing rate as of the reporting date, income and expense with third parties at the average rate for the reporting date range, equity subscribed at incorporation at the historical rate of that date, and an intercompany income or expense line at the rate recorded on its charge date; opening Retained Earnings 3100 is carried forward at the amount already presented rather than retranslated. The residual is presented in **Currency Translation Adjustment 3200** as a separate component of equity at `$11,090.00 USD`, and the remeasurement of a monetary balance stays a distinct event at Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss 7200 in the entity's own books. Neither is netted into Retained Earnings 3100.
- [ ] **IFRS 12 — disclosure of interests in other entities.** The composition of the group the statements cover — each entity with its country of incorporation, functional currency, ownership percentage, consolidation method and the date it entered the consolidation — is read from the configuration behind the run rather than reconstructed in narrative notes, and the translation reconciliation per subsidiary is part of the evidence behind that disclosure.
- [ ] **Double-entry integrity.** Each statement is a read of balanced records: every `account.move` behind it is posted with its own total debits equal to its own total credits at a difference of `0.00` in its company's functional currency, and each of the three elimination entries carries total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `0.00 USD`. That is why an imbalance in the Consolidated Balance Sheet is attributable to the presentation and never to an unbalanced source entry, and why the balance identity is asserted rather than assumed.

## Estimation

| Dimension | Rating | Justification |
|-----------|--------|---------------|
| **Effort** | High | Two statements rather than one, each with its own parameter set, assembled over three entities in three functional currencies and presented in a fourth capacity — USD as the group presentation currency as well as the parent's functional currency. Five measurement bases are applied per run: the closing rate as of the reporting date, the average rate for the reporting date range, the historical rate as of each entity's incorporation date, opening retained earnings carried forward at the amount already presented, and the residual that is not translated at all and lands at Currency Translation Adjustment 3200. On top of the aggregation sit the elimination overlay of three entries, the Intercompany Reconciliation residual disclosure, three no-statement paths, five edge cases, and the retention of an issued pack with the rate provenance behind every line |
| **Complexity** | High | The proof is harder than the presentation. Two identities must hold simultaneously and they can disagree: the Consolidated Balance Sheet must balance at total assets equal to total liabilities plus total equity, and the profit the Consolidated Profit & Loss reports must equal the profit line inside that equity — and a run can satisfy the first while failing the second if a translated result reaches equity at one rate class and the income statement at another. Every presented line then carries a third obligation, that it tie back to the contributing entities' journal items less the posted eliminations, which spans two sets of foreign books, a rate applied at full precision with rounding taken once at the presentation increment, and an overlay that must not double-count. Date-dependent scope membership, the rule that no subset of the scope is ever published, and reproducibility of an issued statement after a rate record changes are each a behaviour to build rather than a check to add |
| **Uncertainty** | Medium | The unknowns are named rather than open: which engine renders the statements and how the drill-down and export path are realized under D-004 and the FEATURE-001-07 convention, whether translated balances are computed on read or materialized per run and how either meets the 60-second rendering target, how the consolidation scope is offered as a report parameter, and where an issued pack and its rate provenance are retained. The entities, rates, account codes, mappings and eliminations all arrive from predecessors and are consumed unchanged. Two programme decisions sit above the story — DEC-001 for the platform version and DEC-002 for the edition source — and neither changes the statement **content**, which is why the story is authorable now; DEC-002 changes only where the presentation is expressed |
| **Story Points** | **13** | Fibonacci scale (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13) |

**Why 13 and not 8.** [STORY-001-06-03](./STORY-001-06-03-define-consolidation-rules.md) is an 8: it **specifies** the mapping, the rate classes and the elimination pairs, and proves each is specified to be consistent, while presenting nothing. This story presents, and everything it presents has to be true at once — two statements over three entities, three rate bases plus two non-translating bases, one group-specific equity component derived as a residual per entity, an elimination overlay of three entries, and the obligation that every line tie back to a named company's sub-ledger. The verification surface is wider than the build: seven acceptance tests, the two tie-out identities asserted independently of one another, the aggregate-to-consolidated reconciliation on both the revenue and the cost line, and a per-subsidiary translation reconciliation that must close at a difference of `0.00 USD` in both the entity's functional currency and USD.

**Why 13 and not 21.** Nothing in the story waits on an unanswered question outside it: the scope, the mapping, the rate classes, the eliminations and the closed entity periods are all supplied by predecessors and consumed unchanged, and the drill-down and export convention is inherited from FEATURE-001-07 rather than designed here. It posts nothing, migrates nothing and reconciles no legacy system. Non-controlling-interest allocation, equity-method accounting and goodwill measurement — the three things that would turn a consolidation into a multi-sprint programme of its own — are outside the Epic's scope, so the equity section is Share Capital 3000, Retained Earnings 3100, the period profit and Currency Translation Adjustment 3200 and nothing more.

**Three identities, three tests.** The balance identity, the report-to-report profit identity and the sub-ledger tie-out are **distinct tests** and none substitutes for another. A statement can balance at total assets of `$3,520,250.00 USD` against total liabilities of `$1,398,000.00 USD` plus total equity of `$2,122,250.00 USD` and still report a profit line that disagrees with the Consolidated Profit & Loss — if a translated result reached equity at one rate class and the income statement at another. Both can agree and both still be wrong against the ledger — if an entity was omitted from the scope, if an elimination was overlaid twice, or if a balance was aggregated at the wrong rate class. `test_consolidated_balance_sheet_balances` therefore asserts the balance identity, `test_consolidated_profit_ties_between_statements` asserts the report-to-report identity, and `test_consolidated_lines_tie_to_entity_subledgers` asserts each presented line against the contributing entities' journal items net of the posted eliminations. All three are required to pass.

## Notes

### Business Context

Before this story the group has four sets of books and no group position. Lenders, investors, regulators and the board each ask for one, and each is answered from a spreadsheet that takes 5 or more business days after the last entity closes, whose elimination working nobody retains, and from which no line can be traced back to the journal item that produced it — so the External Auditor tests the group's numbers by re-performing the consolidation rather than by reading its evidence. The two statements this story renders replace that: the Consolidated Balance Sheet as of 2025-03-31 at total assets of `$3,520,250.00 USD` and the Consolidated Profit & Loss for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 at profit of `$223,360.00 USD`, both produced within 48 hours of the last of the three entities applying its period lock date, both agreeing with each other at a difference of `0.00 USD`, and both drilling down to the named company's journal items. The group's translation exposure stops being an unexplained movement inside retained earnings and becomes a stated equity component of `$11,090.00 USD`, `$1,230.00 USD` of it Global Europe SARL's and `$9,860.00 USD` Global Asia Pte Ltd's. And the `$40,000.00 USD` the group charged itself stops inflating group revenue and group cost, contributing `$0.00 USD` to each.

### The Statements This Story Owns, and the Ones It Does Not

Three reports belong to this story: the **Consolidated Balance Sheet** with an as-of-date parameter, the **Consolidated Profit & Loss** with a date-range parameter, and the **Intercompany Reconciliation** report with an as-of-date parameter, all in the group presentation currency USD. Their names are deliberately distinct from the single-entity **Balance Sheet** and **Profit & Loss Statement** that [FEATURE-001-07](../FEATURE-001-07-financial-reporting-period-close.md) owns and runs for one named company, because the two sets answer different questions and reconcile to different populations: a single-entity statement reads one ledger in that company's functional currency, while a consolidated statement aggregates three ledgers, translates each at the rate class its balance class carries, and overlays the eliminations. Four capabilities that a reader might expect here live there instead and are inherited rather than rebuilt: the comparative-period columns, the statement-line drill-down interaction, the PDF and XLSX export path, and the section-classification convention. What this story adds to that convention is the extra hop a group line needs — from a consolidated figure to the contributing entity balances with the company named, and from an eliminated line at `$0.00 USD` to the elimination leg that removed it.

### Two Statements, One Story

The Consolidated Balance Sheet and the Consolidated Profit & Loss are authored as one story rather than two, and the reason is the tie-out. The profit for the period that the Consolidated Profit & Loss reports is the profit line inside the equity that the Consolidated Balance Sheet presents; each statement is the other's proof, and neither can be accepted alone. They also share the whole apparatus that makes either of them true — the same consolidation scope resolved at the same reporting date, the same rate-class matrix, the same elimination overlay, the same rounding discipline and the same tie-out obligation to the entity sub-ledgers. Splitting them would produce two half-stories whose acceptance criteria each depended on the other's output, and the feature contributes exactly five stories, of which this is the fifth.

### Why a Blocked Run Presents Nothing

Three conditions stop the presentation entirely rather than degrading it: a company in scope holding an unposted entry inside the range, a rate class the policy requires that cannot be resolved, and a registered intercompany pair standing unmatched beyond the `$0.02 USD` tolerance. In each case the count of statements produced is 0 and the count of statements presenting a subset of the scope is 0. The alternative — publishing a group position that omits one entity, or translating one entity at a substitute rate — is worse than publishing nothing, because a partial group statement still balances and still looks complete, so the reader has no way to see what is missing. That is why the refusal names the company and the missing input, and why no subset of the consolidation scope is ever issued as a group position.

An earlier authoring pass of this folder read Global Asia Pte Ltd as 80% owned with a 20% non-controlling interest presented at an equity code of its own, and derived a different investment elimination, translation reserve and consolidated revenue from those figures. ### Open Questions for Refinement

- Whether a re-run of an already issued pack is presented as a new dated version alongside the superseded one or replaces it in place — the first keeps every published figure readable at the rates it was translated at while the second keeps the period unambiguous at a glance, and the choice is settled with the Group Controller and the External Auditor before the build.
- How long an issued pack and its rate provenance are retained, and whether the External Auditor expects to read the pack of a superseded run as well as the one in force.
- Whether the consolidated statements carry a comparative column against the preceding comparable period, which would inherit the comparative convention FEATURE-001-07 owns and extend the translation policy to a second set of rate dates; the worked quarter here is a single period, and adding a comparative is a decision for the Group Controller with the Financial Reporting Manager rather than an assumption of the run.
- Whether an entity's contribution is presented as a column per entity beside the consolidated column, which the External Auditor would read as the consolidation working, or only through the drill-down — the second is the narrower reading recorded here, and a change to it is approved rather than assumed.
- Whether the group ever wants a statement produced while one entity's period is still open, which Scenario 5 refuses outright; the safer reading is recorded, and any relaxation is approved by the Group Controller with the External Auditor informed rather than decided inside the run.
