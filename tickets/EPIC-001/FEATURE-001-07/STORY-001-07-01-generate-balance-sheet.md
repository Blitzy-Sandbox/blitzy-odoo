This is the **statement of position** of FEATURE-001-07, and it is the second of the feature's five stories to be delivered, after the sub-ledger tie-out of [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md). It presents what the entity owns and owes at a single named date: Current Assets and Non-current Assets summing to Total Assets, Current Liabilities and Non-current Liabilities summing to Total Liabilities, and an Equity section whose current-year-earnings line is derived from the income and expense population rather than read from a stored balance. Under IFRS the same statement is titled the **Statement of Financial Position** (IAS 1); under US GAAP it is the Balance Sheet (ASC 210). The two names describe one report, and the report answers one question at one date.

The statement is **read-only against the ledger**. It creates, alters and reverses no journal entry: the posted journal-entry count and every account balance of the reported company and as-of date are identical before and after a run, before and after an export, and before and after a drill-down. Only [STORY-001-07-05](./STORY-001-07-05-execute-period-close-deferrals.md) writes into a period, and the cutoff and closing entries it posts are what the statement is regenerated to include.

Four of its lines are produced elsewhere and are presented rather than computed here, so this story links them instead of restating them — the fixed-asset net book value, the receivable detail, the deferred-revenue balance and the deferred-expense balance. The account taxonomy that decides which section a balance is presented under, together with its IFRS and GAAP mapping, belongs to [STORY-001-01-02](../FEATURE-001-01/STORY-001-01-02-map-accounts-ifrs-gaap-taxonomy.md). The net book value of Fixed Assets **1500** less Accumulated Depreciation **1590** is produced by [FEATURE-001-08](../FEATURE-001-08-fixed-assets-depreciation.md) and posted by [STORY-001-08-03](../FEATURE-001-08/STORY-001-08-03-post-depreciation-entries.md). The detail behind the Accounts Receivable **1200** balance is the Aged Receivable report of [STORY-001-03-05](../FEATURE-001-03/STORY-001-03-05-report-aged-receivables.md), and the Deferred Revenue **2300** and Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400** balances are the remaining schedules of [STORY-001-07-05](./STORY-001-07-05-execute-period-close-deferrals.md). Consolidated presentation across entities, and the closing-rate translation policy that governs it, belong to [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md) and are produced by [STORY-001-06-05](../FEATURE-001-06/STORY-001-06-05-generate-consolidated-financials.md); no criterion here infers a group position by summing entities.

> **Monetary and date conventions used throughout this ticket.** Every amount is stated in USD, carried to 2 decimal places, and rounded half-up at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 (ISO 4217 currency code `USD`). Every percentage variance is stated to 2 decimal places, rounded half-up, and a prior-period balance of `$0.00 USD` presents no percentage rather than a division result. Every date is stated as an explicit calendar date in ISO 8601 form rather than as a relative period. Every account is named by its concept and its four-digit code from the Epic's canonical chart ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)) — Bank **1010**, Accounts Receivable **1200**, Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400**, Fixed Assets **1500**, Accumulated Depreciation **1590**, Accounts Payable **2000**, Tax Payable **2200**, Deferred Revenue **2300**, Share Capital **3000**, Retained Earnings **3100**, Revenue **4000**, Expense **6100** and Depreciation Expense **6500** — together with Cash **1000**, owned by [FEATURE-001-04](../FEATURE-001-04-bank-reconciliation-cash-management.md) and cited here only where a zero-balance account is the subject of the assertion, and Currency Translation Adjustment **3200**, owned by [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md) and cited only where the translation policy is referred to. Every company is named by its registered name and its entity code from the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)). Every tax figure is stated as three separate values: the tax code, the base amount and the tax amount.

## User Story

**As a** Financial Reporting Manager

**I want** the **Balance Sheet** — the IAS 1 Statement of Financial Position — produced from the posted `account.move.line` population of one named company at one named as-of date, presenting Current Assets and Non-current Assets under Total Assets, Current Liabilities and Non-current Liabilities under Total Liabilities, and an Equity section carrying Share Capital **3000**, additional paid-in capital where the entity holds it, Retained Earnings **3100** and a current-year-earnings line derived from the Revenue **4000** and Expense **6100** populations of the open fiscal year, with an optional comparative column for a prior as-of date beside an absolute and a percentage variance, and with every line resolving to the journal items that compose it

**So that** the statutory and investor position of each legal entity is a read of the ledger produced in **under 5 minutes** rather than a compilation that took **2 to 4 hours** of exporting balances and re-keying them into spreadsheets (SM-005); so that a lender, a board and a regulator read a position in which total assets equal total liabilities plus total equity to the cent, evidenced rather than declared; and so that the **External Auditor** answers a question about any line by opening the journal items behind it instead of requesting an extract, which is what lets the close run in 5 business days instead of 10 (SM-003), the statement be published within 24 hours of the lock date (SM-004), and post-close audit adjustments fall by half (SM-016).

## Demonstration Path

The outcome is observable by the Finance Controller and by the Product Owner in a database loaded with the deterministic fixture named in § Test Requirements, without a developer present:

- In **Accounting → Reporting**, with the **Balance Sheet** selected for company **Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`)** and the as-of date 2025-03-31, the statement presents Total Assets of `$4,812,600.00 USD`, Total Liabilities of `$1,612,600.00 USD` and Total Equity of `$3,200,000.00 USD`, so total assets equal total liabilities plus total equity at a difference of `$0.00 USD`, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01.
- Inside those totals, the Bank **1010** line reads `$248,300.00 USD`, the Accounts Receivable **1200** line reads `$2,093,811.88 USD`, the Accounts Payable **2000** line reads `$1,284,300.00 USD` and the Retained Earnings **3100** line reads `$900,000.00 USD`, and the current-year-earnings line reads `$300,000.00 USD`, which is the net income the **Profit & Loss** of [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md) reports for 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 in the same company.
- With the comparative column enabled for the prior as-of date 2024-12-31, the same statement presents prior Total Assets of `$4,567,450.00 USD`, an absolute variance of `$245,150.00 USD` and a percentage variance of `5.37%`, while the current-year-earnings line — whose prior balance is `$0.00 USD` — presents its absolute variance of `$300,000.00 USD` and no percentage.
- Opening the Accounts Receivable **1200** line presents the journal items behind it, whose signed sum taken with the account's opening position is the `$2,093,811.88 USD` the line was opened from, and that figure is the closing balance the **Trial Balance** of [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) reports for the same account, company and date at a difference of `$0.00 USD`.
- The same statement is readable through the Odoo ORM and the external API for the same parameters, so the demonstration does not depend on the screen, and it exports to PDF and to XLSX under the feature convention with every exported value identical to the value displayed.
- Before and after the run, the posted journal-entry count and every account balance of company `US-01` are unchanged, which is what makes the demonstration repeatable.

## Acceptance Criteria

Eight criteria are authored, inside the 4-to-8 bound the Epic sets in [§5.3](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#53-feature-and-story-decomposition-guidelines) and superseding the 3-to-6 guidance carried by the story template, and they carry the mandated coverage distribution: **Scenarios 1, 2, 3 and 4 are the valid-input cases** (the statement produced for a named as-of date with its three totals and the accounting equation; the section placement of every asset and liability line decided by account type, including the Tax Payable **2200** presentation with its tax code, base amount and tax amount held apart; the Equity section with its derived current-year-earnings line; and the comparative column with its absolute and percentage variance), **Scenario 5 is the accounting edge case** (a comparative as-of date inside a period whose lock date has been applied, beside an account whose balance is `$0.00 USD`), **Scenario 6 is the audit-trail case** (a section line resolved down to the journal items that compose it), **Scenario 7 is the invalid-input case** (an as-of date that falls outside every fiscal year defined for the company), and **Scenario 8 is the error-handling case** (an account whose account type is unset, which no section can receive).

Each scenario states its parameters in its **Given** and reduces its **When** to the single act of requesting the statement, or to the single act of opening a line, so no criterion carries two triggers and no criterion depends on another having run first. The superseded balance-sheet story combined the selection of the report and the supply of its as-of date into one trigger; that pattern is not carried forward, and the parameters are a precondition here rather than part of the action.

Three conventions hold across all eight. Every `account.move` contributing to the statement is in state **Posted** with its own total debits equal to its own total credits at a difference of `$0.00 USD`, which is why an imbalance in the statement is attributable to the statement and never to an unbalanced entry. The statement is scoped to the books of the single company named in the criterion, **Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`)**, and a group position is produced only through [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md) and never inferred by summing entities here. And no criterion refers to a screen element or to a code path: each states the parameters, the single act of requesting the statement, and the amounts the statement then presents.

- **IFRS presentation — IAS 1**: the same statement is available under its IFRS title, the **Statement of Financial Position**, and the section captions and their ordering follow the presentation and disclosure requirements of IAS 1. The FASB Accounting Standards Codification and the IFRS Foundation Standards are the frameworks the published set is prepared under, and the account-to-taxonomy mapping that binds them is published by [STORY-001-01-02](../FEATURE-001-01/STORY-001-01-02-map-accounts-ifrs-gaap-taxonomy.md).
- **Double-entry integrity (C-009)**: the statement is a read of balanced records. Every `account.move` behind it is posted with its own total debits equal to its own total credits at a difference of `$0.00 USD`, and total assets equal total liabilities plus total equity at a difference of `$0.00 USD`. - **Currency and date notation**: amounts carry an ISO 4217 currency code and dates are stated in ISO 8601 form, on screen and in every export.
- **Ledger immutability**: the statement creates, alters and reverses no journal entry. The posted journal-entry count and every account balance of the reported company are identical before and after a run, an export and a drill-down.

### Account type to Balance Sheet section mapping

This table is the definite replacement for the vague classification the superseded story carried. It is the single declared mapping the criteria above refer to, and it is consumed by the statement rather than re-decided at each section. The **Carries opening balance** column is what separates a cumulative balance-sheet line from the derived current-year-earnings line: an account type that carries an opening balance is presented at its cumulative position at the as-of date, while the current-year-earnings type carries none, which is precisely why that line is computed from the income and expense populations of the open fiscal year.

| Account type | Internal group | Balance Sheet section | Carries opening balance |
|--------------|----------------|-----------------------|-------------------------|
| receivable | asset | Current Assets | Yes |
| bank and cash | asset | Current Assets | Yes |
| current assets | asset | Current Assets | Yes |
| prepayments | asset | Current Assets | Yes |
| non-current assets | asset | Non-current Assets | Yes |
| fixed assets | asset | Non-current Assets | Yes |
| payable | liability | Current Liabilities | Yes |
| credit card | liability | Current Liabilities | Yes |
| current liabilities | liability | Current Liabilities | Yes |
| non-current liabilities | liability | Non-current Liabilities | Yes |
| equity | equity | Equity | Yes |
| current-year earnings | equity | Equity (Current Year Earnings) | No |

Applied to the mandated chart, that mapping places Bank **1010** under Current Assets on the bank-and-cash type, Accounts Receivable **1200** on the receivable type, Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400** on the current-asset type, Fixed Assets **1500** and its contra Accumulated Depreciation **1590** under Non-current Assets on the fixed-asset type, Accounts Payable **2000** under Current Liabilities on the payable type, Tax Payable **2200** and Deferred Revenue **2300** under Current Liabilities on the current-liability type, and Share Capital **3000** and Retained Earnings **3100** under Equity on the equity type, with the current-year-earnings line derived from the Revenue **4000** and Expense **6100** populations together with Depreciation Expense **6500**.

## Notes

**Export and drill-down are a shared capability, not a separate deliverable.** The feature owns one export and drill-down convention — CAP-X01 in [FEATURE-001-07 §4.1](../FEATURE-001-07-financial-reporting-period-close.md) — and this story together with [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md), [STORY-001-07-03](./STORY-001-07-03-generate-cash-flow-statement.md) and [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) each inherit it rather than restate it: **export and drill-down are a shared capability of `STORY-001-07-01` through `STORY-001-07-04`**, and outside this feature the same convention is inherited by `STORY-001-03-05`, `STORY-001-09-03`, `STORY-001-05-03` and the aged-payables criteria anchored in `STORY-001-02-04`. In practice that means one PDF path carrying the statement title, the company, the as-of date and the other parameters, the generation timestamp and page numbers; one XLSX path writing numeric cells as numbers rather than as text with headers identical to the on-screen labels and each comparative period in its own column; one drill-down from a summary line to the journal items that compose it, where the drilled items sum to the line at a difference of `$0.00 USD` and each item states its journal-entry reference, date, account and amount; filters that persist into the detail view; and breadcrumbs that return to the parent statement without regenerating it. The retired backlog carried this as a standalone story; folding it into the report stories keeps one implementation across all four, and it is why Scenario 6 and the third reconciliation check of the Definition of Done are stated here as the audit trail of this statement's own lines rather than as a general capability.

**The platform target is unresolved and deliberately left so.** This repository is Odoo 19.0 — `odoo/release.py` declares `version_info = (19, 0, 0, FINAL, 0, '')` — while the originating programme request named Odoo 17 and the retired backlog named 18.0. The criteria above are written as accounting outcomes and hold under any of the three, but the report action, QWeb template and field APIs behind them do not, so the target is inherited from the Epic as **DEC-001** and flagged for stakeholder confirmation rather than chosen here. The same applies to **DEC-002**: `account_reports`, the Enterprise reporting module, is absent from `addons/` in this repository, while `addons/account_financial_report_ce` is present at version 19.0.1.1.0 under AGPL-3 with a working `account.balance.sheet.report` model, so the reporting layer is assembled from an Enterprise subscription or from OCA add-ons once the decision is confirmed. Neither decision changes a single figure asserted above; both change the code path that satisfies it, and a confirmed target other than 19.0 also re-opens whether the present Community add-on can be credited at all under D-003.

**Two behaviours of the present Community implementation are corrected rather than inherited.** `addons/account_financial_report_ce/models/financial_report.py` computes a percentage variance of `100.0` when the comparison amount is zero and the current amount is not; the feature convention requires **no percentage** against a `$0.00 USD` prior amount, which is exactly what the current-year-earnings row of Scenario 4 asserts, so that behaviour is changed. And the account-type section groups are currently held as constants on each report model rather than declared once, which is what allows the same account to be presented under different captions on two statements of the same period; the declared mapping in § Technical Discovery Notes is the single rule the Balance Sheet, the Profit & Loss and the Trial Balance grouping consume.

**Which artifact set the figures belong to.** Every entity, date, account balance, variance and tax figure above is drawn from the fixed artifact set of the parent feature — the as-of date 2025-03-31 with 2024-12-31 as the comparative, the closing position the four sibling statements are reconciled against, and the entities of the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)): **Global Holdings Inc. The five foundation stories of FEATURE-001-01 are demonstrated on a separate fixture company pair that the register holds outside the group scope, and no criterion here treats a fixture company as a group entity or reads a figure from that timeline.

**Why the additional-paid-in-capital caption is presented at zero.** IAS 1 and ASC 210 both expect the contributed-capital components of equity to be presented separately, so the caption is part of the statement structure rather than an optional line. Company `US-01` holds no equity-type account beyond Share Capital **3000** and Retained Earnings **3100** in the canonical chart, so the caption presents `$0.00 USD` in the worked set. Presenting the caption at zero rather than omitting it is what keeps the equity structure stable across entities and across periods, and it is the same behaviour EC-2 asserts for a zero-balance asset account.
