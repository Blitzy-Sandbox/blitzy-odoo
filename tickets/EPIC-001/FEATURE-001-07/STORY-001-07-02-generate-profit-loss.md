This is the **statement of performance** of FEATURE-001-07. It answers one question over one span of time: what the entity earned and spent between two named dates, and what result that leaves. The statement presents Revenue, then Cost of Goods Sold, then **Gross Profit** with its **Gross Margin** percentage, then Operating Expenses, then **Operating Income**, then Other Income and Other Expenses, and finally **Net Income**. Under IFRS the same statement is titled the **Statement of Profit or Loss** (IAS 1); under US GAAP it is the Income Statement, or the Profit & Loss in ordinary finance usage. The three names describe one report, and this ticket calls it the **Profit & Loss** throughout.

Its parameter is a **date range and not an as-of date**, and that is a consequence of the chart rather than a presentation choice: income and expense accounts carry no opening balance across a fiscal-year boundary, so there is no cumulative position to read at a single date. Every figure the statement presents is a movement inside the range it was asked for. That is also why its result is reconciled sideways rather than downwards: the Net Income asserted below is the current-year-earnings line of the **Balance Sheet** in [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md) for the same company and period, and the same figure is where the indirect-method **Cash Flow Statement** of [STORY-001-07-03](./STORY-001-07-03-generate-cash-flow-statement.md) begins.

The statement is **read-only against the ledger**. It creates, alters and reverses no journal entry: the posted journal-entry count and every account balance of the reported company and range are identical before and after a run, before and after an export and before and after a drill-down. The only entry this ticket refers to is the fiscal-year closing entry that moves the year's result into Retained Earnings **3100**, which is posted by [STORY-001-07-05](./STORY-001-07-05-execute-period-close-deferrals.md) and is read here only to state that this statement excludes it from the income and expense population it aggregates.

> **Monetary, percentage and date conventions used throughout this ticket.** Every amount is stated in USD, carried to 2 decimal places, and rounded half-up at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 (ISO 4217 currency code `USD`). A journal item held in a currency other than the company currency is presented at its company-currency equivalent with the original currency alongside it, each at that currency's own decimal precision. Two percentage conventions are in force and each is stated wherever it is used: the **Gross Margin percentage** is stated to **1 decimal place**, rounded half-up, because it is a ratio read as a margin; every **variance percentage** is stated to **2 decimal places**, rounded half-up, which is the comparative convention the parent feature sets for the Balance Sheet, the Profit & Loss and the Trial Balance alike. A percentage whose denominator is `$0.00 USD` is presented as `N/A` rather than as `0.0%`, as an infinite value or as a division error. A difference between two percentages is stated in **percentage points**, never as a percentage of a percentage. Every date is stated as an explicit calendar date in ISO 8601 form rather than as a relative period. Every account is named by its concept and its four-digit code from the Epic's canonical chart ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)) — Revenue **4000**, Expense **6100** and Depreciation Expense **6500** carry every figure asserted below, with Retained Earnings **3100** cited where the closing entry is referred to, Accounts Payable **2000** and Tax Payable **2200** where a counterpart is named, and Gain/Loss on Disposal **7210** (owned by [FEATURE-001-08](../FEATURE-001-08-fixed-assets-depreciation.md)) and Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss **7200** (owned by [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md)) where the Other Income and Other Expenses captions are populated. Every company is named by its registered name and its entity code from the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)). Every tax figure is stated as three separate values: the tax code, the base amount and the tax amount.

## User Story

**As a** Financial Reporting Manager

**I want** the **Profit & Loss** — the IAS 1 Statement of Profit or Loss — produced from the posted `account.move.line` population of one named company over one named date range, presenting Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold, Gross Profit with its Gross Margin percentage, Operating Expenses, Operating Income, Other Income and Other Expenses, and Net Income; presenting the expense population either **by nature** or **by function** as two elections of the same population; scopable to an analytic account or an analytic plan; with an optional comparative column for a prior date range beside an absolute and a percentage variance and an optional budget column read from FEATURE-001-09; and with every line resolving to the journal items that compose it

**So that** the period result of each legal entity is a read of the ledger available in **under 5 minutes** rather than a compilation that consumed **40+ hours per quarter** of exporting balances and re-keying them into spreadsheets (SM-005); so that the **Group Controller** reads a margin and a variance that were computed once from posted entries rather than restated by hand; so that the Net Income the board is given is the same figure the Balance Sheet carries to equity, evidenced rather than declared; and so that the **External Auditor** answers a question about any revenue or expense line by opening the journal items behind it instead of requesting an extract — which is what lets the close run in 5 business days instead of 10 (SM-003), the statement be published within 24 hours of the lock date (SM-004), and post-close audit adjustments fall by half (SM-016).

- Switching the expense election from **by function** to **by nature** leaves Gross Profit at `$868,900.00 USD` and Net Income at `$340,000.00 USD` and changes only the labels the `$1,536,550.00 USD` operating-cost population is analysed under — the whole expense population of `$1,542,000.00 USD` less the `$5,450.00 USD` Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss **7200** charge that both elections present below Operating Income — which is the IAS 1 choice made visible in one run.

## Acceptance Criteria

Eight criteria are authored, inside the 4-to-8 bound the Epic sets in [§5.3](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#53-feature-and-story-decomposition-guidelines) and superseding the 3-to-6 guidance carried by the story template, and they carry the mandated coverage distribution: **Scenarios 1, 2, 3 and 4 are the valid-input cases** (the statement produced for a named date range with its captions, subtotals and Gross Margin; the expense population analysed by nature as the second IAS 1 election; the comparative column with its absolute and percentage variance beside the budget column; and the statement scoped to one analytic account), **Scenario 5 is the audit-trail case** (a caption resolved down to the journal items that compose it, with export inherited from the feature convention), **Scenario 6 is the invalid-input case** (a date range whose end precedes its start), **Scenario 7 is the error-handling case** (a range reaching into a fiscal year that has not been defined for the company), and **Scenario 8 is the accounting edge case** (a range crossing a fiscal-year boundary, where the year-end closing entry is excluded so no portion of the range is double-counted).

Three conventions hold across all eight. Every `account.move` contributing to the statement stands in state **Posted** with its own total debits equal to its own total credits at a difference of `$0.00 USD`, which is why a Net Income that disagrees with the equity movement is attributable to the statement and never to an unbalanced entry. The statement is scoped to the books of the single company named in the criterion, **Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`)**, and a group result is produced only through [STORY-001-06-05](../FEATURE-001-06/STORY-001-06-05-generate-consolidated-financials.md) and never inferred by summing entities here. And no criterion refers to a screen element or to a code path: each states the parameters, the single act of requesting the statement or opening one of its lines, and the amounts that are then presented.

### Scenario 1: The Profit & Loss is produced for a named company and date range with its captions, subtotals, Gross Margin and Net Income

## Estimation

| Dimension | Assessment | Rationale |
|-----------|------------|-----------|
| **Effort** | Medium-High | One statement, but 6 parameters, 8 captions, 4 subtotals, 2 expense label sets, a Gross Margin computation, 2 variance columns, an optional budget column, an analytic filter and a fiscal-year-portion rule — each of which is a stated figure that has to be produced and tested. | **Uncertainty** | Medium | Two programme decisions are open and neither changes a figure above: the platform version target (DEC-001) and the reporting-layer edition source (DEC-002), with `account_reports` absent from `addons/` and `account_financial_report_ce` present. The by-function label set depends on a classification the chart carries, which is confirmed with the Financial Reporting Manager during discovery. |
| **Story points** | **8** | Fibonacci. Above a 5 because of the two elections, the comparative and budget columns, the analytic filter and the fiscal-year-boundary rule, each carrying its own assertions; below a 13 because the population is one company's posted ledger, the arithmetic is stated in advance, and the export and drill-down convention is inherited rather than designed. |

### Test Dataset

The deterministic load source is **`test_data/financial_reports/sample_journal_entries.csv`**, recorded as D-009 for this feature and read but never modified. Its columns are `Date, Journal, Reference, Account Code, Account Name, Partner, Label, Debit, Credit`, which is the shape a revenue-and-expense aggregation is demonstrated against: dated items carrying an account, a partner, a label and a debit and credit pair.

Two caveats govern its use, and both are load-time mappings rather than changes to the file:

1. **Account codes are mapped onto the mandated chart.** The file carries 6-digit legacy codes while this backlog's chart is 4-digit, so each row is loaded against the mapped account rather than against the code as written. The crosswalk is recorded in § Notes, and no acceptance criterion above states a legacy code.
2. **The asserted population extends the file.** The rows in the file are dated in fiscal year 2024 and are loaded as that year's movement, including the 2024-01-01 to 2024-03-31 comparative range and the 2024-10-01 to 2024-12-31 portion of Scenario 8; the fiscal year 2025 first-quarter movement asserted in the criteria is seeded from the feature's fixed artifact set for company `US-01` on the same column shape. ## Notes

**Export and drill-down are a shared capability, not a separate deliverable.** The feature owns one export and drill-down convention (CAP-X01 in [FEATURE-001-07 §4.1](../FEATURE-001-07-financial-reporting-period-close.md)), and [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md), this story, [STORY-001-07-03](./STORY-001-07-03-generate-cash-flow-statement.md) and [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) each inherit it rather than restate it. The retired backlog carried it as a standalone story; folding it into the statement stories keeps one implementation of PDF and XLSX export and one drill-down surface across all four, and it is why Scenario 5 and the third reconciliation check of the Definition of Done are stated here as the audit trail of this statement's own captions rather than as a general capability.

**Net Income here and current-year earnings on the Balance Sheet are one figure.** This statement computes the result of a range; the Balance Sheet presents the accumulated result of the open fiscal year as its current-year-earnings line. For company `US-01` and the range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 — a range whose start is the fiscal-year start — the two are the same `$340,000.00 USD`, and the reconciliation is asserted at a difference of `$0.00 USD` in both tickets. They diverge only where the requested range is not the year to date, which is why the reconciliation is stated for a named range rather than in general. At the fiscal-year end the result leaves the current-year-earnings line for Retained Earnings **3100** through the closing entry owned by [STORY-001-07-05](./STORY-001-07-05-execute-period-close-deferrals.md), whose own debits equal its own credits; this statement reads that entry only to exclude it from the income and expense population, which is what Scenario 8 asserts as an amount.

**Why the parameter is a date range and not an as-of date.** Income and expense accounts carry no balance across a fiscal-year boundary, so there is no cumulative position to read at a single date: every figure this statement presents is movement inside the range it was asked for. That single property explains three behaviours asserted above — that no caption presents an opening balance, that a range crossing a fiscal-year boundary is presented as one portion per fiscal year, and that a fiscal-year closing entry inside the range is excluded rather than aggregated.

**The platform target is unresolved and deliberately left so.** This repository is Odoo 19.0 Community, the programme request named Odoo 17 and the retired backlog named 18.0. The criteria above are written as accounting outcomes and hold under any of the three, but the API surface behind them does not, so the target is inherited from the Epic as DEC-001 and flagged for stakeholder confirmation rather than chosen here. The same applies to DEC-002: `account_reports`, the Enterprise reporting module, is absent from `addons/` in this repository, while `addons/account_financial_report_ce` and `addons/account_budget_management` are present, so the reporting layer is assembled from an Enterprise subscription or from OCA and Community add-ons once the decision is confirmed. Neither decision changes a single figure asserted above.
