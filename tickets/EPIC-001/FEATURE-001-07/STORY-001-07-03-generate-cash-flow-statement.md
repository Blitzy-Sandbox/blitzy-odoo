This is the **statement of cash movement** of FEATURE-001-07. The Balance Sheet of [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md) states the position at a date and the Profit & Loss of [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md) states the result over a range; neither answers where the cash went. This statement does, and it answers it in one shape fixed by IAS 7 and ASC 230: three activity sections — **Operating**, **Investing** and **Financing** — each closing on a `Net cash provided by (used in) …` subtotal, followed by the net change in cash, the separately stated effect of exchange rate changes on cash, and the opening and closing cash and cash equivalents that the whole statement reconciles between.

It is produced by **two methods over the same population**, because both standards permit both and a group may be asked for either. The **indirect method** begins at the Net Income the Profit & Loss reports, adds back non-cash charges, removes non-cash gains, and adjusts for the movement in working capital. The **direct method** presents the cash receipts and the cash payments themselves. The two are different derivations of one figure, so they report the **same** net cash from operating activities for the same company and the same range, and that equality is asserted as an amount rather than assumed.

It is the one story of this feature with a **hard prerequisite inside the feature**. The cash flow derives from ledger transaction data: the working-capital movement and the cash receipts and payments it presents are read from the posted journal items the **General Ledger** of [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) presents, and both ends of the statement agree to the Bank **1010** position that story reports. The retired backlog recorded the same dependency, the parent feature carries it forward in its §3.3 ordering, and this ticket states it as a **Blocked By** relation rather than leaving it to be inferred.

The statement is **read-only against the ledger**. It creates, alters and reverses no journal entry: the posted journal-entry count and every account balance of the reported company and range are identical before and after a run, before and after an export and before and after a drill-down. Every entry it aggregates is already posted with its own total debits equal to its own total credits, and the non-cash depreciation add-back it presents corresponds to postings owned by [STORY-001-08-03](../FEATURE-001-08/STORY-001-08-03-post-depreciation-entries.md) that carry that same equality. The only entries this ticket refers to are the period cutoff and the fiscal-year closing entries of [STORY-001-07-05](./STORY-001-07-05-execute-period-close-deferrals.md), and it refers to them to state that they change the balances this statement reads and that the statement is regenerated after they post.

> **Monetary, method and date conventions used throughout this ticket.** Every amount is stated in USD, carried to 2 decimal places, and rounded half-up at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 (ISO 4217 currency code `USD`); the one criterion scoped to a sterling entity states GBP at the GBP rounding increment of 0.01. A cash outflow is presented in parentheses — `($265,000.00) USD` — and is never presented as an unsigned figure whose direction the reader has to infer. A journal item held in a currency other than the company currency is presented at its company-currency equivalent with the original currency alongside it, each at that currency's own decimal precision. The **effect of exchange rate changes on cash and cash equivalents** is a line of its own and is never folded into an activity subtotal or into the net change. The **method** is a run-time parameter with exactly 2 permitted values, `indirect` and `direct`, and it carries no default, so a statement is never produced under a method nobody chose. Every date is stated as an explicit calendar date in ISO 8601 form rather than as a relative period. Every account is named by its concept and its four-digit code from the Epic's canonical chart ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)) — Bank **1010** and Cash **1000** are the cash-and-equivalents population, Accounts Receivable **1200**, Inventory **1100**, Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400**, Accounts Payable **2000**, Tax Payable **2200** and Deferred Revenue **2300** carry the working-capital movement, Fixed Assets **1500** with its contra Accumulated Depreciation **1590** and Investment in Subsidiary **1700** carry the investing activity, Share Capital **3000** and Retained Earnings **3100** carry the financing activity, and Revenue **4000**, Expense **6100**, Depreciation Expense **6500**, Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss **7200** and Gain/Loss on Disposal **7210** carry the result the indirect method starts from and the non-cash items it adjusts. Every company is named by its registered name and its entity code from the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)). Every tax figure is stated as three separate values: the tax code, the base amount and the tax amount.

## User Story

**As a** Financial Reporting Manager

**I want** the **Cash Flow Statement** produced from the posted `account.move.line` population of one named company over one named date range, under a stated method parameter of `indirect` or `direct`, presenting Operating, Investing and Financing activities with a `Net cash provided by (used in) …` subtotal on each, then the net change in cash, then the effect of exchange rate changes on cash as its own line, then opening and closing cash and cash equivalents that the statement reconciles between, where the closing figure equals the Bank **1010** balance the Balance Sheet presents for the range end date, where both methods report the same net cash from operating activities, and where every activity line resolves to the journal items that compose it

**So that** the **Financial Reporting Manager** can state where an entity's cash came from and where it went in **under 5 minutes** rather than assembling it by hand from ledger extracts over 2 to 4 hours per statement (SM-005); so that the **Treasury Analyst** reads a cash movement whose two ends agree with the reconciled bank position rather than a figure that has to be argued for; so that the **Group Controller** applies one interest-classification election and one cash-equivalent policy consistently from period to period instead of restating the basis each quarter; so that the liquidity disclosure IAS 7 and ASC 230 require is delivered as part of the statutory set for every legal entity (SM-001) and published within 24 hours of the lock date (SM-004); and so that the **External Auditor** answers a question about any activity line by opening the journal items behind it — which is what lets the close run in 5 business days instead of 10 (SM-003) and post-close audit adjustments fall by half (SM-016).

## Demonstration Path

The outcome is observable by the Finance Controller and by the Product Owner in a database loaded with the deterministic fixture named in § Test Requirements, without a developer present:

- In **Accounting → Reporting**, with the **Cash Flow Statement** selected for company **Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`)**, the date range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31 and the method parameter set to `indirect`, the statement presents **Net cash provided by operating activities of `$412,300.00 USD`**, **Net cash used in investing activities of `($265,000.00) USD`**, **Net cash used in financing activities of `($90,000.00) USD`**, a **net change in cash and cash equivalents of `$57,300.00 USD`**, an **effect of exchange rate changes on cash of `($5,450.00) USD`** on its own line, **opening cash and cash equivalents of `$196,450.00 USD`** and **closing cash and cash equivalents of `$248,300.00 USD`**, each rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01.
- The three activity subtotals add to the `$57,300.00 USD` net change, and `$196,450.00 USD` plus `$57,300.00 USD` less `$5,450.00 USD` gives the `$248,300.00 USD` closing figure — arithmetic the observer re-adds on the screen rather than takes on trust.
- That closing figure of `$248,300.00 USD` is the Bank **1010** line the **Balance Sheet** of [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md) presents as of 2025-03-31 for the same company, and the opening figure of `$196,450.00 USD` is that statement's Bank **1010** line at 2024-12-31, each at a difference of `$0.00 USD`.
- Changing the method parameter from `indirect` to `direct` for the same company and range leaves **Net cash provided by operating activities at `$412,300.00 USD`** and changes only how that figure is arrived at — a reconciliation from Net Income under the first, a schedule of cash receipts and cash payments under the second — which is the IAS 7 election made visible in one run.
- Requesting the same statement for the sub-period 2025-03-01 to 2025-03-31 presents an effect of exchange rate changes on cash of `$0.00 USD`, so opening cash of `$186,800.00 USD` plus a net change of `$61,500.00 USD` gives the same closing cash of `$248,300.00 USD` with no reconciling item at all.
- Opening the investing line **Purchases of property, plant and equipment** of `($265,000.00) USD` presents the journal items behind it, whose amounts sum to `$265,000.00 USD` — the Fixed Assets **1500** period debit the **Trial Balance** of [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) reports for the same company and range — at a difference of `$0.00 USD`.
- The same statement is readable through the Odoo ORM and over the **C-023 JSON web-service surface** under the named `cash-flow-read` integration principal for the same parameters, so the demonstration does not depend on the screen, and it exports to PDF and to XLSX under the feature convention with every exported value identical to the value presented.
- Before and after the run, the posted journal-entry count and every account balance of company `US-01` are unchanged, which is what makes the demonstration repeatable on any later calendar day.

## Acceptance Criteria

Eight criteria are authored, inside the 4-to-8 bound the Epic sets in [§5.3](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#53-feature-and-story-decomposition-guidelines) and superseding the 3-to-6 guidance carried by the story template, and they carry the mandated coverage distribution: **Scenarios 1, 2, 3 and 4 are the valid-input cases** (the statement produced under the `indirect` method with its 3 subtotals, its exchange-rate-effect line and its closing-cash tie; the same statement produced under the `direct` method reaching the same net operating cash with the tax remittance held apart from its base and tax amounts; a sub-period whose exchange-rate effect is `$0.00 USD`, where opening cash plus the net change gives closing cash with no reconciling item; and an activity line resolved down to the journal items that compose it with the inherited export convention), **Scenario 5 is the invalid-input case** (a date range whose end precedes its start), **Scenario 6 is the incomplete-input case** (the method parameter left unset), **Scenario 7 is the error-handling case** (a company whose chart carries no account of the bank-and-cash type, so no cash-and-equivalents population exists to report on), and **Scenario 8 is the accounting edge case** (a range holding no cash movement, where all 3 subtotals are `$0.00 USD` and closing cash equals opening cash).

The indirect and the direct method are kept in **separate scenario blocks**. They are 2 derivations of one figure and a combined block would hide which derivation failed; separating them is also what makes the equality between them assertable as its own outcome.

Four conventions hold across all eight. Every `account.move` contributing to the statement stands in state **Posted** with its own total debits equal to its own total credits at a difference of `$0.00 USD` — the statement posts nothing itself, so a difference in it is attributable to the statement and never to an unbalanced entry, and the non-cash Depreciation Expense **6500** add-back corresponds to the balanced depreciation postings of [STORY-001-08-03](../FEATURE-001-08/STORY-001-08-03-post-depreciation-entries.md). Each criterion states its parameters in its **Given** and reduces its **When** to the single act of requesting the statement or opening one of its lines, so no criterion carries 2 triggers. Each criterion is scoped to the books of the single company it names, and a group cash flow is produced only through [STORY-001-06-05](../FEATURE-001-06/STORY-001-06-05-generate-consolidated-financials.md) and never inferred by summing entities here. And no criterion refers to a screen element or to a code path: each states the parameters, the trigger, and the amounts that are then presented.

Seven properties of that set are what the criteria assert, and each is arithmetic a reviewer can re-add:

- **The 3 subtotals add to the net change.** `$412,300.00 USD` less `$265,000.00 USD` less `$90,000.00 USD` gives `$57,300.00 USD`, at a difference of `$0.00 USD`.
- **Opening cash, the net change and the exchange-rate effect give closing cash.** `$196,450.00 USD` plus `$57,300.00 USD` less `$5,450.00 USD` gives `$248,300.00 USD`, at a difference of `$0.00 USD`. Read the other way, the movement in cash across the quarter is `$51,850.00 USD`, which is the Bank **1010** variance the Balance Sheet reports between its 2 as-of dates.
- **Both ends are Balance Sheet cash lines.** `$196,450.00 USD` is the Bank **1010** line of [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md) at 2024-12-31 and `$248,300.00 USD` is its Bank **1010** line at 2025-03-31, each at a difference of `$0.00 USD`, and each is also the Bank **1010** opening and closing balance of the **Trial Balance** of [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) for the same range.
- **Both methods reach one operating figure.** The 10-row indirect derivation and the 7-row direct schedule each total `$412,300.00 USD`, at a difference of `$0.00 USD` against each other. The non-cash rows of the indirect derivation — depreciation, remeasurement and the disposal-gain removal — appear in no direct-method line, because the direct method presents cash and those rows carry none.

| **Effort** | Medium-High | One statement, but 4 parameters, 3 activity sections, 31 named line labels, **2 separate operating derivations over one population**, an exchange-rate-effect line, a 4-line reconciliation block, a closing-cash tie to 2 sibling reports and a non-cash supplementary disclosure — each of which is a stated figure that has to be produced and tested. | **Uncertainty** | Medium | Two programme decisions are open and neither changes a figure above: the platform version target (DEC-001) and the reporting-layer edition source (DEC-002), with `account_reports` absent from `addons/` and `account_financial_report_ce` present with a `cash_flow` report object whose coverage is assessed by inspection under D-003. Two caveats govern its use, and both are load-time mappings rather than changes to the file:

**Export and drill-down are a shared capability, not a separate deliverable.** The feature owns one export and drill-down convention (CAP-X01 in [FEATURE-001-07 §4.1](../FEATURE-001-07-financial-reporting-period-close.md)), and [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md), [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md), this story and [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) each inherit it rather than restate it. **Why this story is Blocked By `STORY-001-07-04`.** The dependency is not a scheduling preference; it is what the statement is made of. Every working-capital adjustment of the `indirect` derivation is the movement between an opening and a closing balance — `($93,811.88) USD` on Accounts Receivable **1200**, `$21,511.88 USD` on Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400**, `$65,955.00 USD` on Accounts Payable **2000**, `$29,195.00 USD` on Tax Payable **2200** and `($60,000.00) USD` on Deferred Revenue **2300** — and those balances are exactly what the **Trial Balance** of [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) publishes. Every receipt and payment of the `direct` schedule is read from the Bank **1010** items its **General Ledger** presents, and the `$1,817,383.12 USD` of receipts and the `$1,765,533.12 USD` of payments and non-cash remeasurement are that account's period debit and period credit columns. Both ends of this statement are the Bank **1010** opening and closing balances that report states. A cash flow built before the ledger surface it reads exists would have to derive those balances a second way, and a second derivation of the same figure is the defect the whole feature is built to remove. The parent feature records the same dependency in its §3.3 ordering and places this story fourth in its §3.4 implementation order, after the tie-out and the 2 statements it reads from.

**The exchange-rate effect on cash, and how it stays consistent with the Profit & Loss.** The `($5,450.00) USD` line is the closing-rate retranslation of the foreign-currency portion of Bank **1010** under IAS 21, and it is reported separately from the 3 activity sections because IAS 7.28 and the ASC 230 reconciliation of beginning and ending cash both require it: it changes the carrying amount of cash without being a cash flow. **One event, presented three times, and the three are not a contradiction:** the single `$5,450.00 USD` remeasurement appears as an Other Expenses charge on the **Profit & Loss** (Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss **7200** in net debit), as a **positive add-back** of `$5,450.00 USD` inside the `indirect` derivation because it reduced net income without moving cash out, and as the **negative** `($5,450.00) USD` exchange-rate-effect line in the reconciliation of beginning to ending cash because it did reduce the carrying amount of cash. The sign differs by role, the amount and the account do not, and the reconciliation closes only if all three are present: `$196,450.00 USD` plus `$57,300.00 USD` less `$5,450.00 USD` gives `$248,300.00 USD` at a difference of `$0.00 USD`, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01. Two further consequences are stated so neither has to be inferred. It is added back as a non-cash item in the `indirect` derivation and appears in no line of the `direct` schedule, which is why both still report `$412,300.00 USD`. And the charge itself is carried on **Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss 7200**, not inside Expense **6100**, because the group chart-of-accounts extension registry of [FEATURE-001-01 §7.4](../FEATURE-001-01-chart-of-accounts-fiscal-year.md#74-group-chart-of-accounts-extension-registry) allocates remeasurement and translation of a monetary balance to 7200 and a bank balance is a monetary balance. The arithmetic closes exactly on that split: Expense **6100** of `$1,392,550.00 USD` is the Accounts Payable **2000** period credit of `$1,371,038.12 USD` plus the Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400** amortisation of `$21,511.88 USD`, and the `$5,450.00 USD` on 7200 is the remaining part of the Bank **1010** period credit of `$1,765,533.12 USD`, which is why that period credit exceeds the `$1,760,083.12 USD` of real payments by exactly the remeasurement. The **Profit & Loss** of [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md) therefore presents it below Operating Income under Other Expenses at `$5,450.00 USD` rather than inside either operating caption, so Operating Income reads `$345,450.00 USD` and Net Income `$340,000.00 USD`. That is why the 3 `income_other` accounts — FX Gain/Loss **7100**, Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss **7200** and Gain/Loss on Disposal **7210** — each stand at `$0.00 USD` for this company and range and that statement's Other Income and Other Expenses captions are both `$0.00 USD`, with no contradiction between the 2 tickets. Whether the charge is reclassified from Expense **6100** to Foreign Exchange Gain/Loss **7200** is a **presentation election for the Group Controller to confirm**: it would move `$5,450.00 USD` between 2 Profit & Loss captions, leave Net Income at `$340,000.00 USD`, and change no cash-flow amount, no subtotal and no closing-cash tie asserted above.

**Which account codes are used, and how 7100, 7200 and 7210 are kept apart.** Every account above is named by its concept and its four-digit code from the Epic's canonical chart ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)), which allocates one concept to one code and governs on any conflict with a ticket. Three codes in the `7 Other Income and Expense` range are a frequent source of confusion and this ticket keeps them apart: **7100** is the realized settlement difference, **7200** is the IAS 21 remeasurement and translation difference — allocated to the chart by [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md), which does not make it that feature’s exclusive posting account, and it is where the `$5,450.00 USD` closing-rate remeasurement of the foreign-currency portion of Bank **1010** is carried — and **7210** is the gain or loss on derecognition of an asset owned by [FEATURE-001-08](../FEATURE-001-08-fixed-assets-depreciation.md). The disposal result of EC-3 is therefore written as Gain/Loss on Disposal **7210** and never at 7200, which matches [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md) and the Epic's own correction record. No long-term borrowing code is asserted anywhere above, because the canonical chart carries none: the borrowing lines of the financing section are classified by the non-current-liability account type and a code for such an account is claimed through the chart-extension registry of [FEATURE-001-01](../FEATURE-001-01-chart-of-accounts-fiscal-year.md) before it appears in any criterion.

**The platform target is unresolved and deliberately left so.** Three targets are on record and they are mutually exclusive: the originating programme request named **Odoo 17**, the retired flat backlog named **18.0**, and this repository is **Odoo 19.0 Community**, where `odoo/release.py` declares `version_info = (19, 0, 0, FINAL, 0, '')`. The criteria above are written as accounting outcomes and hold under any of the three; the API surface behind them does not, because the report and QWeb rendering path, the lock-date model that EC-4 reads, and the closing-rate resolution the exchange-rate-effect line depends on have each moved between those releases. The target is therefore inherited from the Epic as **DEC-001** and flagged for stakeholder confirmation rather than chosen inside this story, and the same holds for **DEC-002**: `account_reports` is absent from `addons/` while `addons/account_financial_report_ce` is present at version 19.0.1.1.0 with a `cash_flow` model and a `report_cash_flow` QWeb object, so the reporting layer is assembled from an Enterprise subscription, from the Odoo Community Association add-ons, or from the Community add-ons already present once that decision is confirmed. A target other than 19.0 also re-opens the credit given to the present add-on under D-003, since a 19.0-series add-on is not directly installable on an earlier series. Neither decision changes a single figure asserted above, and neither is resolved here.
