This is the **only story in FEATURE-001-07 that writes into the ledger**, and it is the last of the feature's five to be delivered. [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md), [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md), [STORY-001-07-03](./STORY-001-07-03-generate-cash-flow-statement.md) and [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) read the ledger and change nothing in it; this story posts the period cutoff entries, their reversals where the option is selected, and the fiscal-year closing entry, all in the **Miscellaneous** journal, and it then applies the journal-entry and tax lock dates that make the published result final. **It is therefore the home of the debit-and-credit equality gate for the whole feature on the writing side, and of the rounding-residual rule for every deferral schedule.**

The story compresses four retired stories into one deliverable and extends them with two capabilities neither of them carried: the **fiscal-year closing entry** that rolls the year's result into Retained Earnings **3100**, and the **application of the period lock dates** that concludes the close. The four retired identifiers are named once, in the migration note of § Revision History, and nowhere else; the identifier of this work is `STORY-001-07-05`.

**The close is an ordered loop, and the order is part of the deliverable.** Cutoff and closing entries post first; the Balance Sheet, the Profit & Loss, the General Ledger and the Trial Balance are then **regenerated** so the published figures include those entries; the reconciliation gate is verified against the regenerated statements; and only then is the period locked. Because of the second step this story is **Blocked By** three of its siblings — the statements must exist before they can be regenerated and reconciled — and the reasons are stated per row in § Dependencies rather than left to inference.

> **Monetary, rounding and date conventions used throughout this ticket.** Every amount is stated in USD (ISO 4217 currency code `USD`), carried to 2 decimal places, and rounded half-up at the USD rounding increment of 0.01, unless the criterion names another currency, in which case the same assertions are made at that currency's own rounding increment — EUR at 0.01 for **Global Europe SARL (`NL-01`)**. **Every deferral schedule states where its rounding residual falls: the per-period amount is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places, and the residual between the sum of the rounded period amounts and the deferred total is placed in the final period, so the period amounts of a schedule sum to its total at a difference of `$0.00 USD` and no rounding drift accumulates.** Where a schedule divides exactly — `$24,000.00 USD` over 12 monthly periods at `$2,000.00 USD` each — that residual is `$0.00 USD`; where it does not — `$24,000.00 USD` over 7 periods at `$3,428.57 USD` in each of periods 1 to 6, a subtotal of `$20,571.42 USD`, and `$3,428.58 USD` in period 7 — the residual is `$0.01 USD` and it lands in period 7. Every date is stated as an explicit calendar date in ISO 8601 form rather than as a relative period. Every account is named by the code of the mandated chart: Bank **1010**, Accounts Receivable **1200**, Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400**, Fixed Assets **1500**, Accumulated Depreciation **1590**, Accounts Payable **2000**, Tax Payable **2200**, Deferred Revenue **2300**, Share Capital **3000**, Retained Earnings **3100**, Revenue **4000**, Expense **6100** and Depreciation Expense **6500**. ## User Story

**As a** Chief Accountant

**I want** the period-end close executed as one governed sequence for one named company — a checklist whose every task carries a named owner and a stated piece of evidence; deferral schedules for revenue and for expense that carry a start date, an end date or a period count, a recognition method of straight-line, date-based or manual, a deferred balance-sheet account of Deferred Revenue **2300** or Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400**, a recognition account of Revenue **4000** or Expense **6100**, and one of three states of Active, Completed or On Hold; a period allocation that is previewed before the schedule is activated and that places its rounding residual in the final period; a cutoff run, on demand and on a schedule, that posts one balanced entry per journal in the **Miscellaneous** journal dated the period-end date and refuses any date inside a locked period; an optional reversing entry dated the first day of the following period that exactly offsets its original; a fiscal-year closing entry that moves the year's result into Retained Earnings **3100**; the **Deferred Revenue Recognition Schedule** that reports what remains deferred and what is scheduled next; and the journal-entry and tax lock dates applied once the regenerated statements reconcile

**So that** revenue is recognized in the period the performance obligation is satisfied under ASC 606 and IFRS 15, prepaid and deferred expense is recognized in the period it is consumed under ASC 340-10 with IAS 1 presentation, and a capitalized cost of obtaining or fulfilling a customer contract is recognized under ASC 340-40 and IFRS 15 paragraphs 91 to 104 — so that the period the organization publishes is the period the transactions belong to, the close completes within 5 business days of period end rather than 10 (SM-003), the statement set is published within 24 hours of the lock date being applied (SM-004), every recognized amount traces from the deferred balance on the Balance Sheet back through its schedule to the invoice or bill that created it, and a locked period cannot be re-stated after the auditor has read it (SM-015, SM-016).

## Acceptance Criteria

Eight criteria are authored, inside the 4-to-8 bound the Epic sets in [§5.3](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#53-feature-and-story-decomposition-guidelines), which supersedes the 3-to-6 guidance the story template carries. They hold the mandated coverage distribution: **Scenarios 1, 2, 3, 4 and 8 are valid-input cases** (a single-schedule cutoff, the batch cutoff across both deferral directions with a suspended schedule, the recognition report and its two tie-outs, the reversal option, and the fiscal-year closing entry), **Scenario 5 is the invalid and incomplete-input case** (a schedule whose end date precedes its start date and which names no deferral account), **Scenario 6 is the error-handling case** (a cutoff run aimed at a locked period), and **Scenario 7 is the accounting edge case** (the rounding residual placed in the final period of a schedule that does not divide exactly). Five further edge cases are carried in § Edge Cases rather than as criteria, so the count stays at 8 with the four-into-one compression fully covered.

Each scenario states its parameters in its **Given** and reduces its **When** to a single trigger, so no criterion carries two actions and no criterion depends on another having run first. Four conventions hold across all eight:

- **Every journal entry this story posts is asserted balanced.** Total debits equal total credits at a difference of `$0.00 USD` for each cutoff entry, each reversing entry and the fiscal-year closing entry, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 (C-009).
- **Every entry posts in the Miscellaneous journal** and is dated the period-end date, except a reversing entry, which is dated the first day of the following period.
- **Every criterion names the company whose books are affected.** Unless stated otherwise that company is **Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`)** of the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)), whose functional currency is USD. A group position is produced by [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md) and is never inferred here by summing entities.
- **Recognized-to-date plus remaining-deferred equals the deferred total** for every schedule in every scenario, at a difference of `$0.00 USD`.

### The period-end close checklist

The close is not the cutoff run alone. Each task below carries a named owner and a stated piece of evidence and is retained as close evidence for the **External Auditor**. The order is the order of the loop: sub-ledgers, then cutoff, then the budget review, then regeneration, then verification, then the lock. Tasks 1 to 6 are signed off **before** the lock dates are applied and task 7 applies them, so those seven are the close of this company. Task 8 sits deliberately **after** the lock: under ORD-005 the group intercompany elimination consumes closed and locked entity results, so it is a hand-over this close produces rather than a gate this close waits on — this company's period closes whether or not the other entities have reached their own lock dates, and the elimination is never computed against balances an entity can still restate.

|---|-----------|------------------|
| **EC-1** | A schedule spans two fiscal years — `DEF-2024-0018` runs from 2024-11-01 to 2025-05-31 across the fiscal-year boundary of company `US-01` at 2024-12-31 | The allocation respects the boundary and no period amount straddles it: the 2 periods ending 2024-11-30 and 2024-12-31 recognize `$3,428.57 USD` each, a fiscal-year 2024 total of `$6,857.14 USD` that is inside the fiscal-year 2024 closing entry, and the 5 periods from 2025-01-31 to 2025-05-31 recognize `$17,142.86 USD` in fiscal year 2025, the two years summing to the deferred total of `$24,000.00 USD` at a difference of `$0.00 USD`; the residual of `$0.01 USD` stays in the final period, 2025-05-31, and is not pulled forward to the year boundary; the closing entry of fiscal year 2024 does not close or alter the schedule, whose remaining `$17,142.86 USD` carries forward on Deferred Revenue **2300** with its total and its end date unmoved; and each amount is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 |
| **EC-2** | A schedule is denominated in a currency other than the company's functional currency — EUR 24,000.00 over 12 monthly periods | Two cases are asserted. In company **Global Europe SARL (`NL-01`)**, whose functional currency is EUR, the schedule recognizes EUR 2,000.00 per period, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the **EUR rounding increment of 0.01**, with the residual of EUR 0.00 in period 12, and each cutoff entry posts in that company's books with total debits equal to total credits at a difference of EUR 0.00. (`US-01`)**, whose functional currency is USD, the same EUR-denominated schedule records both the transaction-currency amount and the company-currency equivalent **at each recognition date** — EUR 2,000.00 at 1.0850 USD per EUR gives `$2,170.00 USD` for the period ending 2025-01-31 and at 1.0400 gives `$2,080.00 USD` for the period ending 2025-02-28 — so each cutoff entry balances in USD at that date's rate at a difference of `$0.00 USD`; the EUR period amounts still sum to EUR 24,000.00 at a difference of EUR 0.00 while the cumulative USD equivalent differs from the USD equivalent booked when the source document posted, and that translation difference is identified on the schedule and its treatment is governed by the IAS 21 policy owned by [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md) rather than settled here |

| **EC-4** | The cutoff run is executed a second time for a period that has already been recognized — the period ending 2025-01-31 for company `US-01`, whose batch entry of `$26,170.62 USD` on each side stands posted | The second run creates **0 additional journal entries** and the posted journal-entry count of that company is unchanged; no schedule's recognized-to-date moves, so the revenue recognized for the period stays `$19,000.00 USD` and the expense recognized stays `$7,170.62 USD`; the Deferred Revenue **2300** and Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400** balances are unchanged; the run reports the periods it found already recognized and the schedules they belong to rather than returning an empty result, so a duplicate run is distinguishable from a period with nothing due; each amount is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01; and because a duplicated recognition would be arithmetically balanced and would pass every check except a count, the guarantee is asserted by an entry-count test rather than by a balance test |
| **EC-5** | A preview of the cutoff run is requested, and a schedule is drafted but not activated | The preview presents the debit and the credit per account and the total per journal for the period requested — Deferred Revenue **2300** at `$19,000.00 USD` debit, Revenue **4000** at `$19,000.00 USD` credit, Expense **6100** at `$7,170.62 USD` debit, Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400** at `$7,170.62 USD` credit and a **Miscellaneous** journal total of `$26,170.62 USD` on each side, each amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the USD rounding increment of 0.01 — and creates **0 journal entries**, leaving the posted journal-entry count and every account balance of company `US-01` unchanged; a drafted schedule that has not been activated holds its allocation lines for review and has posted nothing, so its deferred total appears in no report of recognized amounts and contributes `$0.00 USD` to the period; and activating that schedule creates no journal entry either, because only a cutoff run posts |

### Accounting Standards Compliance

- **The standard is applied by deferral type, not blanket (SM-015)**: deferred **revenue** is recognized under **ASC 606** *Revenue from Contracts with Customers* and **IFRS 15**, whose five-step model measures the performance obligation and treats the unrecognized amount as a contract liability, and whose determination of point-in-time against over-time transfer of control decides the recognition pattern a schedule expresses; prepaid and deferred **expense** is recognized under **ASC 340-10** with **IAS 1** presentation; and a capitalized cost of obtaining or fulfilling a customer contract is recognized under **ASC 340-40** and **IFRS 15 paragraphs 91 to 104**. The retired backlog's blanket citation of ASC 606 and IFRS 15 across every deferral type is not carried forward.
- **Double-entry integrity (C-009)**: every entry this story posts — each cutoff entry, each reversing entry and the fiscal-year closing entry — carries total debits equal to total credits at a difference of `$0.00 USD`, asserted as an amount rather than declared as a principle, and every schedule's recognized-to-date plus remaining-deferred equals its deferred total at the same difference.
- **Presentation and framework**: **IAS 1** governs the presentation of the statements the close regenerates, and the FASB Accounting Standards Codification and the IFRS Foundation Standards are the frameworks the published set is prepared under. Amounts carry an ISO 4217 currency code and dates are stated in ISO 8601 form, on screen, in every export and in every retained close artifact.

### Business Rules

- **Lock-date enforcement across all five lock dates**: the fiscal-year, tax, sale, purchase and hard lock dates of the company are each evaluated **before** any entry is constructed. A refused run creates 0 journal entries, names the violated lock date and the next available posting date, and reports the refusal against each affected schedule. The lock dates themselves are defined and administered by [STORY-001-01-05](../FEATURE-001-01/STORY-001-01-05-configure-period-lock-dates.md); this story **applies** them at close and never redefines them.
- **Reversibility of every generated entry**: each cutoff entry, each reversing entry and the fiscal-year closing entry is reversible by the platform's standard mechanism, so a recognition or a close can be undone without a schedule or an entry being deleted, and the reversal is itself a posted, balanced entry rather than a deletion.
- **An audit trail from every entry to its source schedule**: each recognition line names the schedule it came from and the journal entry it posted, each schedule names the invoice or bill it was created from, each state change and each schedule modification records its author and timestamp, and every checklist sign-off and lock-date application is recorded the same way. The **External Auditor** reads all of it without a data request.
- **No new deferral type is invented for the close**: only Deferred Revenue **2300** and Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400** hold deferred balances, and only Revenue **4000** and Expense **6100** receive recognition, with Retained Earnings **3100** receiving the fiscal-year result. The chart is the Epic's canonical register ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)) and no criterion in this file states a code outside it.

### Security and Data Handling

### Allocation methods

| Method | Calculation | Rounding and residual | Use case |
|--------|-------------|-----------------------|----------|
| **Straight-line** | Deferred total ÷ number of periods | Each period amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the currency's rounding increment; the residual between the rounded sum and the deferred total placed in the **final** period — `$24,000.00 USD` over 12 periods gives `$2,000.00 USD` each and a residual of `$0.00 USD`, while `$24,000.00 USD` over 7 periods gives `$3,428.57 USD` in periods 1 to 6 and `$3,428.58 USD` in period 7 | A subscription or a service delivered evenly across the term |
| **Date-based, by calendar days** | Deferred total × (days in the period ÷ total days in the recognition range), with the first and last periods prorated to the days they cover | Each period amount rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at the currency's rounding increment; the residual placed in the **final** period — `$10,000.00 USD` over the 90 days from 2025-01-15 to 2025-04-14 gives `$1,888.89 USD`, `$3,111.11 USD`, `$3,444.44 USD` and a final `$1,555.56 USD` | A term that begins or ends part-way through a period, or months of differing length |
| **Manual** | Amounts entered per period by the Chief Accountant | The entered total is checked against the deferred total before activation and the schedule is not activated while the two differ; each entered amount is stated to 2 decimal places in the schedule currency | A milestone-based contract whose recognition pattern is neither even nor time-proportional |

## Notes

**The close loop is deliberate, and its order is the deliverable.** The cutoff and closing entries this story posts change the balances the statements report, so the order inside a period is fixed and evidenced: the sub-ledgers are posted and reconciled; the checklist review runs the statements as they stand; the revenue and expense cutoff entries post into the period in the **Miscellaneous** journal; the Balance Sheet, the Profit & Loss, the General Ledger and the Trial Balance are **regenerated** so the published figures include those entries; the regenerated Trial Balance difference is asserted at `$0.00 USD`; and only then are the journal-entry and tax lock dates applied, after which a further posting into the period is refused. A statement published before the cutoff is a draft, and the lock date is what marks the difference. This is why the story is **Blocked By** [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md), [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md) and [STORY-001-07-04](./STORY-001-07-04-generate-general-ledger-trial-balance.md) — the statements have to exist before they can be regenerated and reconciled — rather than because any of their code is needed to build a schedule or post an entry.

**The rounding-residual rule, stated in words.** Each period amount of a deferral schedule is rounded half-up to 2 decimal places at its currency's rounding increment, which for USD and for EUR is 0.01. The residual between the sum of those rounded amounts and the deferred total is then placed in the **final** period of the schedule, and no other period is adjusted. A schedule that divides exactly carries a residual of `$0.00 USD`: `$24,000.00 USD` over 12 monthly periods recognizes `$2,000.00 USD` in each, and its residual demonstrates nothing on its own, which is why a second worked figure is carried throughout this ticket. A schedule that does not divide exactly carries a residual of `$0.01 USD`: `$24,000.00 USD` over 7 periods recognizes `$3,428.57 USD` in each of periods 1 to 6, a subtotal of `$20,571.42 USD`, and `$3,428.58 USD` in period 7, the seven amounts summing to `$24,000.00 USD` at a difference of `$0.00 USD`. The rule matters because the alternative — re-deriving the final period's amount from the deferred total at posting time — loses the cent and breaks the schedule-to-ledger tie-out that SM-015 is read from.

**The account-code correction carried in from the retired backlog.** The retired cut-off entry example labelled its deferral account "Deferred Revenue" against a code in the intercompany-payable band of the canonical chart, and its recognition account "Service Revenue" against a code the canonical chart does not carry. In this backlog's canonical chart ([Appendix E.2](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e2-canonical-group-chart-of-accounts)) deferred revenue is **2300**, revenue is **4000**, prepaid and deferred expense is **1400**, expense is **6100** and retained earnings is **3100**; the code the retired example used for deferred revenue is **Intercompany Payable** here, introduced by [FEATURE-001-06](../FEATURE-001-06-multi-company-consolidation.md). Posting a deferral to that code would put a recognition entry into the intercompany elimination population, which is the kind of error that survives every balance check and fails only at consolidation, and the code it used for service revenue would post revenue to an account no entity's chart defines. **Neither retired code is written anywhere in this file — not even as an illustration of the mistake — because a wrong code quoted in a ticket is a wrong code an implementer can copy;** the canonical codes above are the only ones stated. No 6-digit code appears in any criterion either: the 6-digit codes in the crosswalk table below are codes of the retired source system, present only to record what each maps onto.

The same applies to DEC-002: `account_reports`, `account_asset`, `account_budget` and `account_consolidation` are absent from `addons/` because they ship with Odoo Enterprise, so the statements this close regenerates come from an Enterprise subscription or from OCA add-ons once the decision is confirmed. Neither decision changes a single figure asserted above.

**What is already present, and the one gap in it.** `addons/account_deferred_revenue` at 19.0.1.0.0 under AGPL-3, depending only on `account`, already carries a deferral schedule model and a recognition line model, the three recognition methods of straight-line, date-based and manual, a residual assigned to the final allocation line, a cutoff wizard with single, batch, preview and reversal modes that delegates its date check to the company's lock-date violation helper, and a recognition dashboard. It is credited under D-003 and assessed before any bespoke build is authorized. **One behavioural gap is recorded rather than glossed over:** its dashboard-facing status computes **On Hold** from the schedule's own draft state, whereas CF-005 requires On Hold to mean recognition **suspended on a confirmed schedule** whose unrecognized balance stays on Deferred Revenue **2300** or Prepaid / Deferred Expense **1400** and which resumes with a catch-up amount in the period of resumption. The three-state model of this story is therefore not satisfied by that computation as it stands, and closing that gap is inside this story's scope.

**Which artifact set the figures belong to.** Every entity, date, account balance and tax figure above is drawn from the fixed artifact set of the parent feature — the reporting range 2025-01-01 to 2025-03-31, the closing position the four sibling statements are reconciled against, and the entities of the Epic's canonical legal-entity register ([Appendix E.4](../../EPIC-001-enterprise-accounting-odoo.md#e4-canonical-legal-entity-register)): **Global Holdings Inc. (`US-01`)**, whose books every criterion is scoped to unless stated otherwise, alongside **Global Europe SARL (`NL-01`)**, which carries the EUR schedule of EC-2, **Global Asia Pte Ltd (`SG-01`)** and, from its incorporation on 2025-04-01, **Global UK Ltd (`GB-01`)**. The fiscal-year 2025 closing entry asserted in Scenario 8 is the same entry [STORY-001-07-01](./STORY-001-07-01-generate-balance-sheet.md) and [STORY-001-07-02](./STORY-001-07-02-generate-profit-loss.md) refer to and exclude, stated with the identical amounts, so the three files describe one entry rather than three.
